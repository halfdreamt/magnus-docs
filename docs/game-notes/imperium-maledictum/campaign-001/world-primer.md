# World primer — player-safe

What a Voll-literate, Ark-raised Tech-Adept would know. Everything here is
safe for players and player-agents; the GM keeps a larger version. Sources:
core sector chapter, AdMech Player's Guide, and the Voll Adventures primer
(pp. 5–14 only — the gazetteer portion the book itself treats as common
knowledge).

## Your church: the Adeptus Mechanicus of the Macharian Sector

The Cult of Mars in this sector is ruled not from a forge world but from a
voidship: the Ark Mechanicus **Recollections of Rust**, a Dark Age marvel
with the industrial capacity of a forge world, home of the **Ferric
Triumvirate** — three Fabricator Generals who took power during the
Macharian Heresy and never gave it back:

- **Obsidian 5-Penta**, the eldest, dogma incarnate, founder of the
  Macharia Vectorii (the heretek-hunters). Faith expressed through
  augmentation; scripture annotated with command-line diktats.
- **Lathe Axis-438**, "Lady Lathe" — the sector's greatest living
  Explorator and acclaimed master of the noosphere. Adventurous, sociable
  by Mechanicus standards, impatient with doctrine ("doctrine and rote are
  for servitors and students"). Moves on anti-grav and arachnid limbs,
  silently; commands her own frigate, *Pierce the Veil*, which she is known
  to lend to Tech-Priests she deems worthy. Vast finds from her Explorator
  centuries still sit unprocessed in the Ark's vaults.
- **Tolmet Quartic-9**, youngest, the Skitarii master tactician who saved
  Eskutchax during the Days of Blinding. Unity with the wider Imperium, by
  calculation.

A fourth name is spoken quietly: **Peraxa Gnost**, the Triumvir who was
aboard when the Ark was cast into the Warp during the Noctis Aeterna, and
was not aboard when it returned. The records are thin, some say
deliberately so.

The Ark tours the sector in multi-year Explorator Rotations, trailing a
flotilla; its arrival is unannounced by design. Parts of the ship remain
sealed since the Warp-stranding, and all who serve near them are sworn to
discretion. Rust from its decks is lucky. Its machine spirit, they say,
guides favoured crew to corridors no one remembers.

Sects you'd know by name (Player's Guide p19–20): the Cult Archivium
(archivists), the Ferrous Whisper (whispered Martian-independence
radicals), the Filigree Clade (perfectionist artisans), Izzatian Xenarites
(xenos-tech radicals — keep your distance), Lexio Arcani (data-diviners,
banished from the Ark for past misconduct; rumour ties them to Lady
Lathe's protection), Macharia Vectorii (the enforcers — pray they never
say your designation), the Opaline Forge (reclaimers of the bionics of the
dead), Divisio Psybernetica (psykana artificers).

## Your patron: Magos Acquisitor Herazade Vex-Kappa-9

Invictus Acquisitor; formerly of forge world Tran'lo; curator of an
Acquisitor vault aboard *Recollections of Rust*, working the century-deep
backlog of Explorator finds. Motivation: Information. Manner: pragmatic
mentor — demanding, precise, unexpectedly generous with equipment, rarely
present in person. Her teaching: **"Curiosity is not transgression.
Curiosity without containment is."** Her agents investigate rumours,
authenticate discoveries, commune with dormant systems, contain dangerous
finds, and return lost knowledge to proper custody. Recovered technology is
revelation, not invention; xenotech and experimentation need explicit
sanction.

Known Patron provisions (Boons): her once-per-mission **Data-Trawler**
sweep of any connected cogitator bank (disruptive, conspicuous — use with
care); **Tech-Acquisitor** procurement channels (Rare comes easier; Exotic
can be *requested*); an **Augmetics Bay** (no availability hurdles, free
surgery, quality work); and **Void Logistician** — the cell's Arvus
Lighter and its retained pilot. Assume the GM knows things about her you
don't. That is normal. That is the game.

## Your world (for now): Voll

Hive world, Cytheris Subsector, colonised by Rogue Trader Halion Castyx
late in the Macharian Crusade; the only habitable world of its system;
over one hundred billion souls. Its bio-rich acidic slurry feeds the
sector's chemical and medicae production — Persepolis (sector capital) and
Macharia (shrine world of the Saint) both depend on it. Governor: Jasper
Castyx. Police: the Macharian Vigilites (hive-core only; they never go
into the wastes).

- **Hive Rokarth**, the capital: a colossal spider shape astride a web of
  mud-sluicing pipes; ~30 billion; largest producer of healing unguents in
  the sector. Mid-levels are washed with scouring acid floods on
  Administratum timetables — wrong floor, wrong time, near-invariably
  fatal. Arrivals from orbit come through the Void Buttress, by Arvus
  Lighter. The Mechanicus HQ is **Forge Fane Incalcos-6**.
- Other hives: Dalal (Nethecaré), Amlyr (Castyx Regent since House Kalveo's
  fall), Vishavar (Taj; periodically attacked by colossal acynadi; Knight
  House Vandensarg rotates through), Sanksorak (Vylathi; source of the
  Ghasts of Voll penal legion).
- **The wastes**: the Unsea's acid silt flats; nomadic Stilt-fleets and
  Wastelanders; a 36-hour day divided into Flowing / Tranquil / Unquiet
  tide-watches synced to the acynadi. Unfiltered air eats lungs; walkers
  beat boats; guilds (Mercator Carnem/Dilaquo/Lenimen) matter more than
  gangs.
- **The acynadi**: Voll's only native fauna, fist-sized to Knight-sized,
  tinned into ration cans across the sector; a standing mystery even to
  Incalcos-6's Biologis.
- **The ruins**: rarest sight on Voll — ancient foundations washed up and
  swallowed again by the acid tides. Technoarcheologists argue pre-Imperial
  human; others argue xenos. Nobody knows. The hives themselves stand on
  infrastructure "dating back tens of millennia" that the stabilisation
  serfs broke into long ago.

## The noosphere (what your training says)

A rare, sacred data-environment surrounding sufficiently advanced
Mechanicus technology — not a universal network. Simple single-function
devices have none. To perceive it at all requires a Noospheric data-tether,
an augmetic "unlike any other," installed under mystery rites. Connection
is an act of will (Discipline); moving unseen and contesting control are
craft (Tech (Security)); Binharic Rites prepare the way. Damaged machines
sound like error-choruses; great systems are cathedrals of data; a Fumble
severs you painfully and angers the spirit. Scholars dispute the
noosphere's origin — most say a Dark Age invention recovered from ancient
Terra; a radical few whisper it has always existed, like the Warp, and the
ancients merely built doors. Believing the second thing too loudly is how
adepts get reassigned.
