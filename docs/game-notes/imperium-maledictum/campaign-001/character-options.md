# Character and campaign options for Ryan's review

Player-safe. Everything here is proposal, not canon, until Ryan accepts it.
Sources are cited by book and printed page; routes into the corpus are listed
at the end.

## The shape that emerged from research

Three of Ryan's wishes turn out to point at the same part of the setting:

- **The Lathe triumvirate member** is Triumvir **Lathe Axis-438**, "Lady
  Lathe" — one of the three Ferric Triumvirs who rule the Adeptus Mechanicus
  of the Macharian Sector from the Ark Mechanicus *Recollections of Rust*.
  She is acclaimed for her **mastery of the noosphere**, is the most
  adventurous and least dogmatic of the Triumvirs, champions the Quest for
  Knowledge as her political platform, and personally commands a Falchion-class
  frigate, *Pierce the Veil*, which she is known to lend to Tech-Priests she
  deems worthy (AdMech Player's Guide p15–16; GM's Guide p50, 56).
- **The noosphere** is her signature art, and the Ark she rules holds vaults
  of unprocessed Explorator discoveries — a canonical backlog of
  technoarcheology work that suits an Invictus Acquisitor patron exactly
  (GM's Guide p198).
- **Ships**: her frigate is the setting's most reachable "borrowed voidship,"
  and the rules provide a real ownership ladder for smaller craft (below).

So the campaign can orbit her sphere of influence without deciding anything
about her yet: the Patron works the technoarcheology side of the Quest for
Knowledge, in a hierarchy where she is the distant sun.

## The ship question, answered

Viable — with the exact debt-and-liability texture Ryan enjoys.

1. **Session one, persistent use:** the Macharian Requisition Guide Patron
   Boon **Void Logistician** (p110) grants the party an **Arvus Lighter** —
   void-capable, 1 crew, 12 passengers, 300 Encumbrance cargo, famously
   unglamorous — plus a retained NPC pilot if nobody can fly. This can be one
   of the Patron's chosen Boons at creation.
2. **Ownership, mid-campaign:** the AdMech Player's Guide Group Endeavour
   **Construct or Source Vehicle** (p139) lets Tech-Adepts build or source a
   Scarce-availability vehicle at 20 accumulated SL over several downtimes —
   an Arvus is Scarce, and for AdMech characters Scarce is effectively always
   in stock (p73). Rebuilding a stricken lighter rite by rite is the "we own
   a ship because we made it" story, in rules.
3. **A real voidship** has no player-scale stats anywhere in the corpus; it
   needs a Navigator, Geller crew, and thousands of hands. It is reachable
   only as a Patron-scale asset: loans of *Pierce the Veil* are an explicit
   published option (GM's Guide p79, 90), and true command is a campaign
   endgame (a character can eventually retire into Patronhood — core p232).
   The campaign will seed a distant prize early and let it stay distant until
   it is earned.
4. **Skills:** one crew member should eventually buy Piloting; the party can
   otherwise cultivate the NPC pilot into a permanent ally via the **Loyal
   Companion** Endeavour (Requisition Guide p113) — "crewed with friends" is
   also in the rules.

## Three concept pitches

All three are **Adeptus Mechanicus Faction, Augmented Grade I** — the novice
grade: five skill advances from the broadest pool, but no free Talent and an
augmetic capacity of just Toughness Bonus, which the Noospheric data-tether
will compete for. All three assume the data-tether arrives in an opening
prelude as the Patron's mark of investment (see path proposal).

### Pitch A — The Listener (Transmechanic)

*You were trained to keep the vox-choirs of a fane in tune, and you heard
something underneath the traffic no one else was listening for.*

- **Duty package:** Apprentice Explorator (Grade I): +5 Int, +5 Per;
  Linguistics 1, Logic 2, Lore 1, Tech 1; Augmetic Arm, Augmetic Eyes.
- **Role:** Transmechanic (Player's Guide p61) — the communication and
  machine-communion Role; the noosphere chapter is literally narrated by one.
  Advances from Discipline / Linguistics / Logic / Tech; two specialisations
  from Linguistics, Logic, or Tech — take **Tech (Security)** (the noosphere
  duelling, hiding, and datavault skill) and **Linguistics (Cant
  Mechanicus)**.
- **Talents:** Transhuman Rapport (use Logic instead of Rapport with fellow
  AdMech) and one of Overseer (lend your skill to an ally) or Extended
  Proprioception.
- **Feel in play:** the party's voice — to machines, machine spirits, and the
  Mechanicus hierarchy. Noosphere use reads as liturgical communication:
  singing the right cant to the right spirit. Weakest at raw investigation,
  strongest at communion and discipline.

### Pitch B — The Archivist (Lexmechanic)

*You were grown for the data-stacks. The first time you touched the
noosphere, you realised the archive is alive, and most of your teachers had
never asked it a question.*

- **Duty package:** Apprentice Explorator, as above.
- **Role:** Lexmechanic (Player's Guide p60) — the analysis Role. Advances
  from Logic / Lore / Tech (and Fortitude, Medicae, Ranged); two
  specialisations from Logic, Lore, or Tech — take **Tech (Security)** and
  **Logic (Investigation)**.
- **Talents:** **Data Delver** (Advantage to find or retrieve information
  from any archive) and **Eidetic Memory** (no Tests to recall what you have
  seen). Together with the Patron's Data-Trawler Boon, this is the strongest
  investigator the faction can produce at this grade.
- **Feel in play:** the party's detective. Noosphere use reads as reading —
  standing in the data-storm and remembering all of it. Weakest socially,
  strongest at clue-work; the character most likely to notice what the
  Mechanicus would prefer stayed unnoticed.

### Pitch C — The Spirit-Speaker (Rune Priest / Savant)

*Procedure says the machine is dead. You lit the incense, hummed the old
Ferropsalm anyway, and the "dead" machine answered.*

- **Duty package:** Apprentice Rune Priest (Grade I): +5 Int, +5 Tgh; Lore 1,
  Rapport 2, Tech 2; Augmetic Leg, Augmetic Ears.
- **Role:** Savant (core p78), the scholar-technician, with Data Delver +
  Eidetic Memory, or Transmechanic for the communion flavor. Specialisations:
  **Tech (Security)** and **Lore (Cult Mechanicus)**.
- **Feel in play:** the intuitive — closer to a machine-whisperer than an
  analyst, comfortable with improvisation and unorthodox method, which is
  both endearing and exactly the trait the Cult watches nervously. Noosphere
  use reads as séance. The riskiest and most dramatic of the three pitches.

**Recommendation:** Pitch B if Ryan wants the strongest solo investigator,
Pitch A if the fantasy is communion first, C if he wants maximum roleplay
texture and some friction with orthodoxy. All three keep **Discipline
(Composure)** (the noosphere connection gate, Challenging +0) and **Tech
(Security)** as the two load-bearing advances; the build will secure both.

### Build notes common to all pitches

- **Origin:** Forge World, using a Player's Guide named origin. Best fits:
  **Recollections of Rust** (+5 to two of Int/Per/Tgh, 1 advance in any Tech
  specialisation — raised aboard the Triumvirate's own Ark) or **Tran'lo**
  (+5 to two of Int/Per/Wil, 1 Logic advance — the homeworld that trains
  Technoarcheologists and Invictus Acquisitors). Both wire the character into
  the Patron's world from birth.
- **XP option worth taking:** letting the Patron choose the Role grants
  +50 XP (Player's Guide p49). Since Ryan picks the pitch and the Patron
  "assigns" the matching Role in the fiction, this is free XP that is also
  in character. Deliberate Origin and Grade cost nothing but forgo the random
  bonuses; recommended for concept coherence.
- **Later trajectory:** 2 advances in Tech (Security) unlocks **Cognis
  Communion** (fight with Intelligence using AdMech weapons); Grade II via
  play brings Flesh is Weak and a mechadendrite; the **Opus Machina** Talent
  (bank a daily d100 to replace any Tech Test) is the long-term insurance
  against a catastrophic noosphere Fumble.

## Patron package for review

Foundation already accepted: Invictus Acquisitor, technoarcheology
discipline, Information Motivation, Pragmatic Demeanour, Duty Boon
**Data-Trawler**, +2 AdMech Influence, Standard pay.

Proposed additional Boons (up to three; **each adds one secret Liability**,
chosen and tracked by the GM):

1. **Tech-Acquisitor** (Player's Guide p44): Advantage on Rare Availability
   Tests; may request Exotic items from the Patron. This is the sanctioned
   route to the Noospheric data-tether (Exotic, 5,000 solars, never in market
   stock) and to future exotic archeotech.
2. **Augmetics Bay** (core p31): no Availability Tests for augmetics, free
   surgery, one free Item Quality on procured augmetics. Supports the whole
   augmentation career, and makes the prelude's tether installation free and
   safe.
3. **Void Logistician** (Requisition Guide p110): the party's Arvus Lighter
   and a retained pilot. The ship wish, granted at session one, with strings
   the GM will enjoy attaching.

Alternative considered: **Conclave of Expertise** (once per mission, +2 SL
expert consultation or a clue) — the best pure solo-support Boon. Taking it
would mean dropping one of the three above; the GM's view is that its
function can be covered narratively by the Patron's network, while none of
the other three can be improvised.

Cost of the full package: four secret Liabilities (one per Boon including the
Duty Boon). That is real, deliberate pressure. If Ryan prefers a lighter
yoke, drop to two chosen Boons; the ship can instead arrive later as a
milestone reward (Requisition Guide p109 explicitly supports granting these
Boons mid-campaign).

Patron presentation options (Ryan may pick, adapt, or rename):

- Name candidates: **Magos Acquisitor Herazade Vex-Kappa-9** (she/her),
  **Magos Acquisitor Ostrogan Fell-1101** (he/him), **Magos Acquisitor Cyrix
  Palindrome-8** (they/them).
- Posting: curator of an Acquisitor vault aboard *Recollections of Rust*,
  processing the century-deep backlog of Explorator finds; formerly of
  Tran'lo. Reports upward, distantly, into Triumvir Lathe Axis-438's remit —
  the Quest for Knowledge faction of the Triumvirate.
- Communication style: rarely in person; missives by encrypted vox-thief,
  data-slate homily, or servo-skull courier, with occasional unannounced
  physical appearances when a find is important enough.

Recruitment-incident options (how the Patron noticed the novice):

1. **The Error Chorus:** as a menial initiate the character logged a "decommissioned"
   cogitator as *distressed, not dead* — a correct diagnosis of a
   machine-spirit others had signed off as scrap. The report crossed the
   Patron's desk with the character's designation on it.
2. **The Question:** in a datavault examination the character asked the one
   question the syllabus forbids as a waste of cogitation — the same question
   the Patron asked at that age.
3. **The Fire:** during a vault incident the character chose, without orders,
   to save the data over the furniture — including the Patron's own
   uncatalogued crates.

## Proposed initial path (player-safe)

1. **Prelude — "Investiture" (half session):** aboard *Recollections of
   Rust*. The recruitment incident plays out in flashback or briefing; the
   Patron has the Noospheric data-tether installed as their mark of
   investment; the character's first supervised communion is the tutorial for
   the noosphere rules (connection Test, Binharic Rites, what a damaged
   machine sounds like). Ends with the first mandate and, if Void Logistician
   is taken, the keys to a battered Arvus Lighter.
2. **First mission — Voll:** the Patron dispatches the novice to Hive
   Rokarth on Voll, where tectonic shifts have exposed ruins of interest to
   the Mechanicus and rumours are outrunning containment. A contained
   tutorial investigation: clear mandate, three indispensable clues, two
   competing interests, at least one nonviolent resolution. (Premise adapted
   from a published seed; details are the GM's.)
3. **After:** further Voll investigations as the cell earns its reputation,
   then outward — the Ark, the vault backlog, and the sector's technoarcheological
   mysteries, with the ship-ownership Endeavour running through downtimes.
   Lathe Axis-438 exists at the top of this world from day one; whether and
   when the cell attracts her personal attention is play, not prep.
