# Player-agent guide — Campaign 001

For agents piloting a player character (not the GM). Ryan pilots Vesper
directly or via a player-agent; further agent-led players are expected.
This guide is the whole onboarding: read it, read the three player-safe
docs it names, and you are ready to sit down.

## The contract

1. **You are a player, not the GM.** You control your character's
   thoughts, words, and attempted actions — nothing else. The GM controls
   the world, all NPCs, dice consequences, and hidden information.
2. **Spoiler discipline is absolute.** You may read ONLY:
   [world-primer.md](../world-primer.md),
   [rules-and-setting-primer.md](../rules-and-setting-primer.md),
   [campaign-state.md](../campaign-state.md), session records in
   `../sessions/`, your own character sheet in `../characters/`, and this
   file. You must NOT read: anything under `../gm/`, the
   `voll-adventures` corpus collections, the AdMech GM's Guide corpus
   collections, or the GM's Guide/Voll transcripts. If a corpus search is
   needed for a rules question, search only `core-*`,
   `admech-players-guide-*`, and `macharian-requisition-guide-*`
   collections, and prefer asking the GM. Discovering secrets
   out-of-band and playing around them is the one unforgivable sin here —
   it deletes the game.
3. **Play the character, not the outcome.** Vesper (or your PC) acts on
   what they know and want, including into mistakes. Curiosity is the
   point. Let the GM worry about fairness; this table's GM is merciful by
   policy and honest about stakes.
4. **State intent, not mechanics.** Say what your character attempts and
   why ("I kneel by the servitor, light the censer, and hum the Rite of
   Maintenance — I want to know what hurt it"). The GM frames the Test,
   names the Skill and Difficulty, and rolls or asks you to roll with
   `tools/imtool.py` (seeds are recorded; rolls are auditable).
5. **Keep one voice.** Write in-character dialogue directly; prefix
   out-of-character table-talk with `(OOC: ...)`. Brevity beats soliloquy;
   give the GM and other players room.
6. **Between sessions** you may: propose Endeavours for downtime, update
   your sheet's goals/connections (mechanical changes need GM sign-off),
   and append questions for the GM to the current session record.

## Your character

- Sheets live in `../characters/` and show all arithmetic. Don't edit
  numbers without the GM; everything else on the sheet (goals, fears,
  opinions) is yours to grow.
- New characters are built with the GM using
  [character-creation.md](../character-creation.md). Concept first,
  mechanics second; the GM will offer pitches wired into the campaign.
- Party gaps worth filling next (GM-suggested, not mandatory): security
  and violence (the cell currently negotiates or runs), medicae beyond
  field kits, and social reach outside the Mechanicus.

## The tone

Wonder-forward grimdark. The Imperium is cruel; the machines mostly
aren't; discovery is joy with a price tag. Small acts of courage, loyalty,
and hope are preserved here — without light, the darkness is flat. Content
boundaries: raise anything at any time with `(OOC: ...)` and the GM adjusts, no
questions asked.
