## Why this campaign works: the Lathe gap

Ryan likes Lathe Axis-438 from her Player's Guide presentation. That
presentation is her cultivated public image, and the GM's Guide deliberately
contradicts it:

- PG p16 (player-safe): she "refrains from radical technical innovations"
  despite temptation.
- GG p57 (GM-only): she has "begun dabbling in the forbidden undertaking
  known as innovation and… shows no signs of stopping." Her other secrets:
  she partakes in the Silent Trade of xenos artefacts (Necron and Aeldari
  especially); her charitable gifts of Tech-Priests to Rogue Trader ventures
  are a personal spy web; she is secretly sympathetic to and in contact with
  the Vasarii heretek cult (GG p190); she quietly shelters the banished
  Lexio Arcani sect aboard *Pierce the Veil* (GG p16); she fears psykers and
  wears experimental anti-psyker augmetics (GG p12, p57).

**The campaign's long question is whether Ryan's "curious but never
heretical" adept can stay both, inside the gravitational field of a patron
saint of curiosity who is quietly past the line.** Never resolve this early.
Lathe should be exactly as admirable in play as the Player's Guide says —
generous, brilliant, anti-dogmatic — for a long time before any thread pulls.

Her tenet, one of the three the Ferric Locum must balance: "Forward the
Quest for Knowledge… Data must be gathered wherever possible" (GG p53). The
Invictus Acquisitor's other duty — scouring the Mechanicus itself for
heretical elements (PG p36) — is the collision course, wired in from
session one but invisible.

Play her with the Artisan stat-block (GG p204) if she ever rolls dice
(GG p57). Voice: declarative "Fact:/Interrogative:" constructions (GG p56);
never threats — leverage, procurement, and curiosity.

Antagonist pressure when needed: Triumvir Obsidian 5-Penta and the Macharia
Vectorii (GG p54–55), who hate what she represents; Procurator Kleo Theta is
"Obsidian's hound." Tolmet Quartic-9 runs statistical analyses on replacing
her (GG p59). The Ferrous Whisper wants the whole Triumvirate gone (PG p19).

## Secret Patron decisions (provisional until Boons are confirmed)

If Ryan accepts the proposed package (Data-Trawler + Tech-Acquisitor +
Augmetics Bay + Void Logistician), the secret Liabilities are:

| Boon | Secret Liability | How it plays |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Data-Trawler (Duty) | **Meticulous Archivist** (core) | Full written mission reports or docked pay. Gentle, characterful, and it generates the campaign's paper trail — reports the player writes become documents that can later be subpoenaed, leaked, or quoted back. |
| Tech-Acquisitor | **Hidden Adherent: Lexio Arcani** (PG p46) | The Patron secretly belongs to the data-heresy sect that considers classification of data a sin — the same sect Lathe Axis-438 secretly protects (GG p16). Sect members appear between missions asking favours. This is the act-three fuse: the Patron's exotic procurement pipeline runs through the sect, and the sect runs under Lathe's protection. |
| Augmetics Bay | **Obsolete Observer** (PG-adjacent, core-legal) | A malfunction-prone chronicler servo-skull documents the cell "for the archive." Comic surveillance that is also, eventually, evidence. |
| Void Logistician | **Filed in Triplicate** (MRG p111) | The Arvus exists on a requisition slip that is never quite in order. Each downtime, paperwork Tests or the next requisition is delayed. The ship is theirs in fact and never quite theirs on paper — exactly Ryan's stated taste for debt/liability texture. |

Rationale: one bureaucratic, one conspiratorial, one comedic, one
administrative; none punishes the noosphere concept itself, and all four
generate story rather than subtraction. If Ryan drops to two Boons, keep
Hidden Adherent (it is the arc) and Filed in Triplicate (it is the ship).

The Patron is **not** a heretek and should never feel like a trap. Their
Lexio Arcani adherence is idealism — "knowledge sequestered is knowledge
lost" — which is also how it stays sympathetic when discovered.

## Campaign arc skeleton

**Act 1 — Voll (sessions ~1–8).**

1. *Prelude "Investiture"* aboard *Recollections of Rust*: tether
   installation, first communion tutorial, mandate, the Arvus.
2. *Original tutorial mission — the Forge-Temple of the Tides*, built from
   the GG p39 seed: tectonic shifts on Voll exposed a pre-Imperial
   forge-temple ruin; stilt-fleet Wastelanders are picking it over and have
   found a chemical that separates clean water from Voll's acid slurry.
   Design target from campaign-state.md applies: three indispensable clues,
   two competing factions (Forge Fane Incalcos-6 vs. the stilt-fleet, with
   hive-noble buyers circling), one Superiority-earning opportunity, one
   nonviolent resolution (the stilt-fleet are sympathetic; the "monster" is
   a wounded machine-spirit the adept can hear). First noosphere set piece:
   the ruin's dormant systems as a drowned choir.
3. *Illicit Fabrications* (VA p48–69) — the most AdMech-native published
   Voll adventure: missing Tech-Priest, wrecked manufactorum, parallel
   Skitarii investigation from Incalcos-6. Runs almost unmodified.
4. Optional third Voll job: *The Eye of Voll* (VA p15–47) **for its
   aftermath**: if the Eye is not fully recovered, it is bartered "to
   underhanded members of the Adeptus Mechanicus" through a noble house —
   the campaign's first thread into the Silent Trade, which later leads
   upward. Even if fully recovered, an Incalcos-6 magos quietly asks who
   *would have bought it*, planting the same thread.

Voll technoarcheology mystery to keep unresolved: the ruins washed up by
the acid tides are canonically unexplained (VA p12 — pre-Imperium human vs.
xenos-progenitor theories, "the truth remains a mystery"), and the acynadi
are speculated to be "parasites to a much larger organism living far beneath
the surface" (VA p14). Reserve these as the campaign's own long mystery —
no published book will contradict whatever we decide later.

**Act 2 — Recollections of Rust and the sector (sessions ~9–20).**

- Recall to the Ark; vault work in the Explorator backlog (GG p198:
  "many discoveries have sat undisturbed for a century, most thought to be
  inactive and forgotten" — a bottomless mission generator).
- *Wayward Warriors* (GG p198–199) as **Lathe's entrance**: she voxes the
  cell directly, bypassing the Patron's authority, to stop a maniple of
  override-compromised Skitarii heading for Acquisitor Vault Alpha-XIII —
  one of *her* vaults. Aftermath: the target device is already gone; a
  shipping record names Explorator Sultus Phi-12. She offers no apology to
  the Patron. The Patron's reaction (and the Meticulous Archivist report
  Ryan must write about it) starts the triangle.
- Optional: Explorator Macharia reagent-hunt missions (GG p88–89) as
  standalone jobs if the campaign wants the sector tour; the six-mission
  short form fits Campaign 001 better than the full campaign.
- Ship arc: Construct or Source Vehicle Endeavour (PG p139) across
  downtimes; Repair Bay berth negotiations; the pilot hireling becomes a
  Loyal Companion (MRG p113). Seed the **derelict Endeavour-class light
  cruiser** (GG p71) in an augur sweep — visible, coveted, impossible for
  now. That hull is the act-three ship prize.

**Act 3 — the collision (open).**

- The Silent Trade thread (from Voll) and the Lexio Arcani thread (from the
  Patron's secret) converge on the same protector. The cell — now trusted by
  the Patron, marked by Lathe, and owning a hull with a disputed paper trail
  — is the natural instrument when someone (Obsidian's hound? an
  Inquisitorial informant? the Ferrous Whisper?) demands the Mechanicus be
  scoured from within.
- Design intent, not scripted outcome: Ryan's adept should face the choice
  between curiosity-with-containment (the Patron's creed) and
  curiosity-unbound (Lathe's secret), with the ship and crew as the thing
  they stand to lose. All endings stay open, including ones where Lathe is
  never exposed, is protected, or elevates the cell.
- Deep-mystery reserve: the Xenarite heresy that "the noosphere has always
  existed, akin to the Warp" and ancient Terran technology merely accesses
  it (PG p129) — if the campaign ever needs a cosmological floor under the
  adept's gift, it is this question, and the Voll ruins can be its evidence.

## Solo-play adjustments

The corpus has no official solo guidance (searched exhaustively). Levers:

- Patron NPC network substitutes for Conclave of Expertise if it was not
  taken; the retained Arvus pilot and later Loyal Companions round out the
  action economy.
- Familiars and hirelings (PG p112–115; MRG p97–102) are the sanctioned way
  to thicken a thin party; a servo-skull or tech-thrall can carry the
  medicae kit.
- Tune encounters: fewer troops, Resolve prominent, nonviolent endings
  always live. Superiority earned in investigation is the solo PC's main
  combat equalizer; be generous with it for good preparation.
- Fate is the true hit-point pool at one PC; telegraph lethality before
  spending it becomes necessary.
