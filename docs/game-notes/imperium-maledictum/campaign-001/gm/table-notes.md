# Table notes — GM-side observations

Running notes on the player(s): style, preferences, motivations, learning
goals. Purpose: continuity for the GM and briefing material for future
player-agents. Not secret in the spoiler sense, but GM-maintained; update
after each session or notable beat.

## Conventions (agreed 2026-08-14)

- Vesper: he/him. OOC prefix: `(OOC: ...)`.
- **Speech translation protocol:** Ryan is learning in-universe idiom. The
  GM takes his dialogue in spirit, renders it into proper Mechanicus
  register before NPC-agents see it, and provides coaching feedback OOC as
  play proceeds. Ryan intends to graduate to writing his own cant "very
  soon" — keep offering small, digestible lessons (honorifics, idiom,
  ritual forms) and celebrate correct usage when it appears.
  **MANDATORY (Ryan, 2026-08-14): every translated line must be quoted to
  Ryan word-for-word, in the moment, marked as canon dialogue — before or
  alongside the NPC's response.** He must always know exactly what his
  character said, since NPCs react to specific wording. Dice results may
  shape the translated delivery (e.g. a failed Test degrades precision);
  say so explicitly when it happens.
- **NPC subagents:** recurring NPCs get persistent briefs in
  `npc-briefs/`; brief interactions may be delegated to small
  (Haiku/Sonnet-class) subagents that receive ONLY their character's
  scoped knowledge plus the translated player dialogue. The GM weaves
  their returns into narration and updates the brief's state line after.
  "Everyone a PC in a sense" — Ryan's phrase; honour it.

## Ryan / Vesper — observed style

- Session 1: First instinct in a social bind was *face-saving diplomacy* —
  credited the overseer's own stamp with the anomaly so investigation
  wouldn't require the overseer to have been wrong. Rewards: NPCs respond
  well to being given ladders to climb down; keep offering social
  geometry, not just skill gates.
- Wants to learn: honorifics, manners, rituals — treat lore-coaching as a
  desired reward channel, not homework.
- Stated loves (pre-campaign): exotic gear and tech, "finding something
  and being able to use it, or figure it out. Unlocking powers from the
  past." Ship ownership with friends aboard. Mercy as a table value.
- Watch for (to be updated in play): risk appetite, combat interest,
  preferred pacing.
