# Session 1 — Investiture, and the Forge-Temple of the Tides

GM-only prep. Prelude (~half session) + Mission 1 (1.5–2 sessions).
References: canon.md C11–C16, weave.md T1/T2/T5, GG p39 (Aqua Regium seed),
VA pp. 7–14 (Voll primer), campaign-state.md design target.

## Part 0 — Investiture (prelude, aboard Recollections of Rust)

Purpose: meet Herazade; install the tether; tutorialize the noosphere;
hand over the Arvus; end with the mandate. Beats:

1. **Cold open, in memory:** the Error Chorus (canon C14), played as a
   short flashback scene the player acts in — young Vesper on the Rustfall
   deck, the "dead" cogitator bank Theta-Kappa-77, the overseer's scorn,
   the choice to file the report anyway. One Test at most (Awareness
   (Sound) or Intuition, Easy +40 — this scene is not failable, only
   colourable). Establish the sound: a chorus singing one wrong note,
   patiently, for years.
2. **Summons:** a chronicler servo-skull (introduce **Muniment** — the
   Obsolete Observer, played as comic-solemn; it documents *everything*,
   badly) leads Vesper through the Ark: the Rustfall, the Foundry of All
   Cogs, up past a Skitarii checkpoint that already has their designation.
   Use one Encounters-table sight (GG p78) for texture.
3. **Herazade, in person** (rare — signal that this matters): Acquisitor
   Vault Alpha-XIII, aisles of century-old Explorator crates. Her
   catechism, conversational, testing: what is recovered technology?
   (Revelation.) What is curiosity? (Sacred.) What is curiosity without
   containment? (Transgression.) She offers the compact: independence,
   equipment, her name when needed — for disciplined reporting, preserved
   evidence, and containment protocol, always.
4. **The Investiture:** surgery in her Augmetics Bay — the tether install
   played as ritual, not procedure (mystery rites unique to the forge
   world, PG p97; here, unique to the Ark). First supervised communion:
   she has Vesper connect (Discipline (Composure), Challenging +0, +1 SL
   Master Crafted) to a benign target — a devotional music-loom — then to
   the *vault itself* for three seconds: a cathedral of sleeping
   catalogue-spirits, one of which turns toward Vesper before she severs
   the link. "That is why containment," she says. Teach: Binharic Rites
   grant Advantage; Fumbles sever painfully; simple functions are free,
   deep functions cost Tests.
5. **The mandate and the keys:** Voll assignment (below), the Arvus
   Lighter **"Sacristan's Patience"** (battered, devout machine spirit,
   smells of incense and bilge-chem) and its retained pilot — **Enginseer
   Third-Class Podra Vellum-Kite** (she/her: chatty, superstitious,
   loyalty currently Professional; Loyal Companion candidate, MRG p113).
   The requisition slip is, of course, imperfect (Filed in Triplicate —
   do NOT fire this liability in session 1; let the paperwork be a joke
   before it is a problem).

XP: 40 for the prelude (mirrors GG p94 prologue award).

## Mission 1 — The Forge-Temple of the Tides

**Mandate (from Incalcos-6, routed through Herazade):** tectonic shifts in
the Unsea have exposed a ruin — apparently a forge temple from Voll's
early Imperial colonisation. Stilt-fleet salvagers are picking it over and
have recovered a chemical that separates clean water from Voll's acid
slurry. Investigate, authenticate, quarantine; recover a sample and the
source before the black market does. (GG p39, adapted; the ruin's truth is
canon C5 — it is NOT early-Imperial, and that discrepancy is the
mission's soul.)

**The truth (GM):** the ruin is a node of the Drowned Choir — Dark Age
noospheric relay, ten millennia older than its "colonial" facade (early
colonists built *onto* it, which is why it reads Imperial at a glance).
The "chemical" is its maintenance medium, still being synthesised by a
living system. The node is damaged and *calling* — which is why the
acynadi in the region are agitated, and why, at the tide's turn, vox and
cogitators nearby stutter in chorus. A noosphere-sighted adept who
connects will hear the choir: vast, wounded, singing maintenance psalms in
a dialect older than the Cult. Nothing here is hostile unless mishandled;
everything here is dangerous if mishandled.

**Factions in play:**

- **The Stilt-fleet band** (Wastelander family, ~a dozen, leader **Matriarch
  Ousel Brine-Aunt**): they found it, they're poor, the water-chemical is
  *life* to them. Sympathetic; they've lost one diver to "the singing
  room" (unconscious, feedback shock — alive, which the party can
  discover and treat). They will bargain, hide things, and fight only if
  cornered.
- **Forge Fane Incalcos-6** (client): wants quarantine and custody;
  dispatch liaison is a by-the-book Lexmechanic, **Auditor-Adept
  Sabine Rho-11**, who arrives late with two Skitarii and paperwork.
- **The buyer** (T2's first rung): a Mercator Lenimen chem-guild factor,
  **Vasco Truel**, is already on the flats with armed hirelings, bidding
  for "the water-maker." He'd rather buy than bleed; he answers, three
  rungs up, to Hive Amlyr money. If he escapes with even a sample, thread
  T2 lights.

**Three indispensable clues** (each with 2+ routes: given, found, or
coaxed):

1. **The facade lie:** colonial-era plating is bolted over stonework whose
   machining marks match nothing in the Imperial pattern archive — the
   ruin is far older than the colony. (Routes: Tech (Engineering) on the
   walls; Lore (Cult Mechanicus) on the "founding" inscriptions; the
   Matriarch's diver saw the under-layer.)
2. **The living synthesis:** the chemical isn't stored, it's being *made* —
   feed-lines run down, deeper than any colonial structure goes. (Routes:
   Logic (Investigation) on the residue gradients; following the acynadi,
   which drink at the outflow; Data-Trawler on the ruin's shallow
   cogitation shell if the party burns it here.)
3. **The chorus:** the "haunting" that felled the diver is a noospheric
   presence — damaged, patterned, *responsive*. (Routes: Vesper's tether —
   passive overlay reveals it from the threshold; the diver's babbled
   testimony; instruments glitching in tide-synced rhythm.)

**Set pieces:**

- *The Unsea crossing* (walker or the Arvus low and slow; tide-watch
  timing; first Superiority opportunity for good preparation — scouting
  the tide tables at Incalcos-6, +1 Superiority per the Know Thy Foe
  pattern).
- *The singing room:* the node's heart. Vesper's first true communion
  set-piece: Challenging (+0) Discipline (Composure) to connect; then
  layered options — soothe the damaged spirit (Rapport via Tech framing,
  or Rite of Maintenance then Tech), map the deep feed-lines (Tech
  (Security)), or ask it *what it is* (open door to C5's first fragment:
  an image of Voll before the hives — towers in a clean sea — then the
  choir falls shy). A Fumble here severs painfully and wakes the region's
  acynadi for the finale. **Do not stat the Choir as an enemy.**
- *The confrontation:* Truel's buyers and the Stilt-fleet's need collide
  at the outflow as the Unquiet tide rises and Knight-mass acynadi surface
  in the middle distance (dread, not combat — yet). Nonviolent resolutions
  are live: buy Truel off (he sells his silence cheap NOW, expensively
  later), out-paper him (Sabine Rho-11's quarantine writ + Vigilites'
  absence is a lovely irony), turn the Stilt-fleet into Incalcos-6's
  sanctioned wardens of the site (the merciful ending: they keep clean
  water, the Mechanicus keeps custody, Vesper keeps a friend-network in
  the wastes). If it goes loud: Truel's six hirelings (Ganger/Enforcer
  stat-blocks), Resolve low, Superiority decides it fast.

**Aftermath ledger:** custody of the site and one sealed sample to
Incalcos-6 (+1 AdMech Influence if clean); the Matriarch as Contact; Truel
escaped/bought/broken (record — T2); the Choir fragment recorded in
Vesper's Meticulous-Archivist report (Herazade reads it very carefully —
her sect would *love* to ask the ruin questions; log the liability beat,
do not fire it yet); XP per GG p82 model (40 base + up to 40 conduct).

**Wayward Warriors bridge (act 2 tease):** in the mission debrief,
Herazade notes the recovered sample and the ruin's noospheric signature
have been "flagged for the Triumvirate's survey of anomalous assets." The
flag's routing code, if anyone looks, terminates at Acquisitor Vault
Alpha-XIII's oversight registry — Lathe's office. The sky has noticed.
