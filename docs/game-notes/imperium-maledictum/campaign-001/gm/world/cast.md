## The Ferric Triumvirate (GG p50–59)

### Triumvir Lathe Axis-438, "Lady Lathe" (GG p56–57; PG p16 ☼)

- ☼ Greatest living Explorator of the sector; noosphere master; anti-grav +
  arachnid legs, moves silently, hovers behind her Tech-Adepts unnoticed;
  argues openly with her peers; "doctrine and rote are for servitors."
- Wants: *Recollections of Rust* freed from its pilgrimage to become a mobile
  Explorator harbour; the Quest for Knowledge above all; to drag her fellow
  Triumvirs "beyond dogma."
- Secrets: Silent Trade in Necron/Aeldari artefacts; spy web disguised as
  gifted Tech-Priests to Rogue Trader colonies; **dabbling in innovation**
  since the Noctis Aeterna "and shows no signs of stopping"; innate fear of
  psykers, wears experimental anti-psyker tech (built by Divisio
  Psybernetica). Public face (PG p16) claims she refrains from innovation —
  the gap IS the campaign.
- Edges: *Pierce the Veil* (her Falchion frigate, cmd. Thaddeus Calpurnik,
  torpedoes beyond repair; Lexio Arcani clade quartered aboard); Rogue
  Trader houses (named her Lady Lathe); Navis Imperialis (owed her for nova
  cannon warhead plans; refurbished her frigate for astrogation data);
  Infractionist consortiums incl. the Trackless Chain; the Vasarii
  (clandestine contact, GG p190); Praefect Acquisitor Antae Rade (she
  created his title); Logis Cyanolyca Windward (lost friend, returning with
  a Warp data cache, GG p49); former Sacristan mentor to Knight Houses of
  Veritas Maximal; usurped her Triumvirate seat.
- Voice: "Fact:… Interrogative:…" constructions; never threatens; hates not
  knowing things first. Stat-block: Artisan (GG p204).

### Triumvir Obsidian 5-Penta (GG p54–55; PG p17 ☼)

- ☼ Eldest; age unknown even to himself; arrived with the Macharian
  Crusade as master of forge vessel *Motive Benediction*; founded the
  Macharia Vectorii; scripture quoted with command-line diktats.
- Wants: doctrine, purity, the eradication of tech-heresy; sees himself as
  spiritual guardian of the other two.
- Secrets: **fears he is dying**, burning through life-extension ever
  faster; **periodically deletes his own memories** for plausible
  deniability — does not remember all the Vectorii did, nor that he founded
  Divisio Psybernetica VI (psychic-bionics heresy) nor the Opaline Forge's
  Omnissiah-body project he commissioned (intends to take the perfect body
  himself, GG p18). Never resolved his view of Electro-Priests; ignores
  them.
- Edges: Macharia Vectorii via **Procurator Kleo Theta** ("Obsidian's
  hound"); Inquisition alliances; the Minotaurs' shadow (his era's
  suppression); rumoured to hate Lathe "almost as much as he did Peraxa
  Gnost" and met Gnost shortly before her death (PG p17 rumour).
- Note the irony available at long range: the Vectorii's Pentactors now
  believe *he* may be impure and are preparing "the grandest prosecution
  yet" (GG p17).
- Stat-block: Logis (GG p205).

### Triumvir Tolmet Quartic-9 (GG p58–59; PG p17 ☼)

- ☼ Youngest (~150); Skitarii master tactician; hero of the Eskutchax
  blockade against Legion Omneros; presses for Imperial unity.
- Secrets: privately targeting Warpsmith Omneros, using Astra Militarum
  allies without Mechanicus knowledge; runs replacement-analyses on his
  fellow Triumvirs (Obsidian's Vectorii reliance −0.412% cohesion; Lathe's
  voyages −0.31–0.36% military presence). **Sector Lord Aulus Sejanus
  secretly engineered his ascension** (PG p13) — proof would incense the
  other Triumvirs.
- Voice: mathematically justified pragmatism; ignores talk beneath his
  concern. Stat-block: Magos (GG p205).

## The Ferric Locum thread (GG p52–53, 80–91)

- **Archmagos Plouton** (former Ferric Locum, ~100 years in post; GG p83):
  Eskutchax orphan, Logis genius. Secretly **Ferrous Whisper**. Calculated
  the Ark's fall within a century and civil war after; resolved to restore
  Eskutchax as capital forge world. Built **Project FL-826-Alpha** (scrapcode
  purge machine) from a heretek's tome in the Archivium's forbidden
  archives. His hand-picked Tech-Adepts fell to **Vashtorr the Arkifane**;
  they tried to seize the machine; he repelled them by venting his own
  workshop into the Warp (with parts of himself — he had integrated much of
  his body into the machine). A workshop section containing "large amounts
  of himself" is **lost somewhere in the sector**. His hope: a successor
  radical enough to finish the work.
- **The Arkifane's Scions** — his four surviving corrupted Tech-Adepts:
  **Kharo Thannek, Nikx Eta, Hydroz Holtz, Xtyx-49** (GG p90). Recurring
  antagonists; want the machine to open a Warp rift on Eskutchax for
  Vashtorr.
- **Ketheros, the lost Tech-Adept** (GG p84): Opal VII / Opaline Forge
  origin; recruited by Plouton for rule-bending augmetic repairs; framed and
  abandoned by the future Scions on Penance VI; stripped of bionics; a
  decade among Infractionists keeping the prison's lights on. Recruitable
  (Mission VIII); loyal as a means to prove himself better than Plouton;
  buried fury reserved for the Scions.
- **Kuiper-967G** (GG p85): Plouton's Warp-cursed multitask servitor, last
  witness; long-term repair project that leaks clues (helped build Plouton
  INTO the machine).
- **Ferric Locum office mechanics** (GG p52–53): all-three-unanimity to
  appoint; judged after each mission by incense-exhaust (red = continue,
  black = replaced); Boons: Office of the Ferric Locum (substitute AdMech
  Influence), Ferric Locum Privilege (one aid request per Triumvir per
  campaign); Liability: Duty of the Ferric Locum (Patron may alter mission
  objectives to keep Triumvir favour). Three tenets, one per Triumvir;
  satisfy two of three per mission.
