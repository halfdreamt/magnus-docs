# Canon decisions — GM-only ledger

Last updated: 2026-08-14. Every optional, ambiguous, or contradictory
element the campaign has ruled on. Format: **ID — ruling** (source; blast
radius). Statuses: CANON (ruled), OPEN (deliberately undecided), TABLE
(needs Ryan/table input before it can matter). Check here before ruling on
anything the books leave open; add new entries the moment a ruling is made
in play.

## Timeline and frame

- **C1 — Date.** Campaign 001 opens circa 012.M42, some years after the
  Noctis Aeterna receded from the sector. Kept deliberately fuzzy; the
  fiction uses rotation legs, not year-stamps. (Core p258; blast radius:
  session records should date by rotation leg + local time.)
- **C2 — Position.** The campaign opens in the closing years of Explorator
  Rotation #46-DK-451, with *Recollections of Rust* working the loop that
  ends near Port Optimar (Persepolis/Voll/Macharia region). (GG p86, p89.)
- **C3 — Plouton lives (for now).** Archmagos Plouton is the sitting Ferric
  Locum through Acts 1–3; his workshop catastrophe and the start of
  rotation #47 occur when the table is ready for Act 4, not before. His
  Tech-Adepts (future Scions) are active in the background gathering
  FL-826 materials. (GG p83–91; blast radius: any published content that
  assumes the new Locum must be re-dated or deferred.)
- **C4 — Scion cameos.** Pre-fall Scions may appear unnamed in Acts 1–3
  (Thannek as a visiting Transmechanic consultant; Eta as a rival bidder
  with off-pattern bionics). Never name the cult before the attack.
  (GG p90, p180.)

## World truths

- **C5 — The Drowned Choir.** Voll's unexplained ruins (VA p12) are CANON
  as a buried Dark Age human noospheric relay network; the acynadi are its
  engineered maintenance fauna (the "much larger organism" of VA p14 is the
  network); the Aqua Regium chemical is its maintenance medium (GG p39).
  The hives were built on its foundations (core p280). Evidence, if ever
  understood, supports the Xenarite noosphere cosmology (PG p129) — making
  the truth theologically explosive. Reveal in fragments; full picture is
  endgame material.
- **C6 — Peraxa Gnost.** Her death during the Warp-stranding was a genuine
  daemonic incursion (GG p63) — no murder. BUT: shortly before, she had
  discovered Divisio Psybernetica VI and confronted Obsidian; he
  partitioned away his memory of that meeting (consistent with GG p12,
  p55). He therefore cannot prove his own innocence, and the
  "intentionally scrubbed" records (PG p15) are his doing without his
  knowledge. The Ferrous Whisper feeds the murder rumour (PG p17).
- **C7 — The Eye of Voll aftermath.** Whether or not the Eye is recovered
  in play, Voll's noble-house Silent Trade conduit (Hive Amlyr, VA p10)
  attempts to broker comparable finds upward; the ladder (weave T2) exists
  independent of any one adventure's outcome. (VA p38 area.)
- **C8 — Resolve threshold.** Superiority ≥ Resolve causes Desperate (core
  p198 detailed rule), over the p197/p318 summaries. Implemented in
  imtool.py. (Also listed in campaign-state.md and retrieval README errata
  E2.)
- **C9 — Augmented Grade II/III text.** The reconstruction in
  `sources/wh40k-imperium-maledictum/retrieval/README.md` errata E1 is
  canon for play: Grade II = Flesh is Weak + single augmetics +
  mechadendrite; Grade III = Transhuman Ideal + paired augmetics.
- **C10 — Data Delver effect.** Until checked against the physical book
  (extraction gap, errata E4): Advantage on Tests to find or retrieve
  information from a document or archive, analog or digital.

## Patron (Magos Acquisitor Herazade Vex-Kappa-9)

- **C11 — Identity.** She/her; formerly of Tran'lo; curator of Acquisitor
  Vault Alpha-XIII's backlog aboard *Recollections of Rust* (GG p198);
  reports upward, distantly, into Lathe Axis-438's Quest-for-Knowledge
  remit. Invictus Acquisitor Duty expressed through technoarcheology;
  Information Motivation; Pragmatic Demeanour. Voice: precise, warm under
  the precision, homilies delivered by data-slate; communicates by
  encrypted vox-thief, servo-skull courier, and rare unannounced
  appearances. Creed: "Curiosity is not transgression. Curiosity without
  containment is."
- **C12 — Boons.** Duty Boon Data-Trawler + Tech-Acquisitor + Augmetics
  Bay + Void Logistician (MRG boon, PG p31 allows core/supplement picks;
  MRG p109 confirms creation-time selection). +2 AdMech Influence,
  Standard pay.
- **C13 — Secret Liabilities** (one per Boon; GM-only; rotate, never
  stack): Data-Trawler → Meticulous Archivist; Tech-Acquisitor → **Hidden
  Adherent: Lexio Arcani** (the act-3 fuse); Augmetics Bay → Obsolete
  Observer (chronicler servo-skull "Muniment", malfunction-prone);
  Void Logistician → Filed in Triplicate. Liability activations logged per
  session in insights.md housekeeping.
- **C14 — Recruitment: the Error Chorus.** As a menial initiate aboard the
  Ark, Vesper logged decommissioned cogitator bank Theta-Kappa-77 (a
  Warp-scarred section adjacent to Habitation Aelph-2) as *distressed, not
  dead* — a correct machine-spirit diagnosis their overseer had signed off
  as scrap. The report crossed Herazade's desk with Vesper's designation
  attached; she ran the numbers on how rare that hearing is, and moved.
  (Builds on GG p61 "the Ark's machine spirit guides favoured crew" — the
  Ark noticed Vesper first. Long thread to the Heart of Rust, T7.)

## Player character

- **C15 — Vesper.** Adept **Vesper Antiphon-6**; he/him (Ryan, 2026-08-14). Origin: Forge World —
  *Recollections of Rust* (PG named origin). Faction: Adeptus Mechanicus,
  Augmented Grade I, Apprentice Rune Priest duty package. Role: Savant,
  assigned by the Patron (+50 XP, PG p49). Characteristics: rolled 2d10+20
  ×9 with rearrangement (+25 XP), seeded and recorded in the sheet.
  Noospheric data-tether: installed in the Investiture prelude as
  Herazade's mark (Tech-Acquisitor procurement + Augmetics Bay surgery,
  free, one Item Quality). Full arithmetic: ../../characters/vesper.md.
- **C16 — The tether's Item Quality.** Master Crafted (Augmetics Bay's
  free quality): +1 SL to the Discipline (Composure) connection Test. A
  gift that whispers of how much was spent on a novice.

- **C17 — Rites and connection.** A Binharic Rite spoken specifically to
  prepare a noospheric connection (e.g. Supplication of Elucidation as a
  petition to be admitted) grants Advantage on the Discipline (Composure)
  connection Test, in addition to the book's Advantage on the subsequent
  Tech Test. Books-adjacent house rule (PG p122, p131 grant Rite Advantage
  only to Tech Tests); adopted 2026-08-15 so the fiction of prayer and the
  dice agree. Process: every PC Test declaration names its modifiers and
  passes them explicitly to imtool (`--advantage`, `--sl-modifier`) so any
  roll can be audited against its declaration.
