# Factions, sects, and networks — GM-only

Last updated: 2026-08-14 (initial build from GG deep read, chunk 1).
Player-safe versions of the sects are PG p19–20; everything below includes
GM-only truth.

## The Ferric Triumvirate (rulers)

Three Fabricator Generals ruling the sector Mechanicus from the Ark
*Recollections of Rust* since the Macharian Heresy — meant to be temporary,
never stood down (GG p6). Backed by most sects and Fabricator Generals, but:
the **Synod of Mars** openly believes they hold too much power and waits for
them to stumble — unseating them would need "evidence of the utmost heresy"
and risk civil war (GG p6). Faith wavered during the Noctis Aeterna; tithes
are behind sector-wide (GG p9). Individual Triumvirs: see cast.md.

Campaign stance: the party's Patron works under their authority via Lathe
Axis-438's Quest-for-Knowledge remit. Every senior AdMech NPC should have an
answer to "how do they feel about the Triumvirate?" (GG p7 explicitly
directs this).

## The eight Macharian sects (GG p10–18)

Rollable d100 table exists (GG p10). Each entry: public face → GM truth →
campaign use.

### The Cult Archivium (GG p11, p19–21)

Stewards of the **Archivium Technis** (see gazetteer). Devoted archivists
and noospheric mystics; recruit Tech-Priests suited to centuries of
cataloguing. Memory-erasing chems administered to all visitors; people who
learn too much either lose their memories or "disappear." Data-informant
network across every sector holding; members appear in the field, retrieve
one seemingly insignificant scan or servitor, and leave.
**Campaign use:** ideological rival-but-not-enemy of the Lexio Arcani
(GG p10: they compete "without working towards each other's destruction").
Hook "Psychic Breach" (GG p11): noospheric mystic Theodosis Task wants
psykers who glimpsed the Archivium neutralised — and secretly wants to study
memory-procedures on psykers.

### Divisio Psybernetica VI (GG p12)

Public face: the Psybernetica divisions build psykana collars, psychic
hoods, Geller repairs — trusted specialists (they built **Lathe's
anti-psyker augmetics**). GM truth: Division VI was founded by **Obsidian
5-Penta to create bionics that induce and control psychic awakening**, then
he erased his own memory of founding it ("If it failed, he would never
know"). Human test subjects from manufactorum masses; most "fail
spectacularly, consumed from within." The Macharia Vectorii have "already
caught their scent" — a bomb with a lit fuse.
**Campaign use:** the sector's most explosive secret shy of the Opaline
Forge's. Links Obsidian to provable tech-heresy — the "evidence of utmost
heresy" the Synod of Mars is waiting for.

### The Ferrous Whisper (GG p13)

Martian-independence radicals: unseat the Triumvirate, restore rule to Mars,
even break the Treaty of Mars. Loose anonymous network; grew during the
Days of Blinding (proof forge worlds can stand alone). Two live strategies:
elevate one of their own to Triumvir and dismantle from within, or
assassinate a Triumvir to make a vacuum. Callsign: "When does Phobos rise
above Olympus Mons?" / "At the hour of our independence."
**Campaign use:** already canon-adjacent to our arc — the late Archmagos
Plouton (former Ferric Locum) was secretly one (GG p83). Hook "Protocol
Plot": a plan to assassinate Tolmet Quartic-9 by overwriting his macroclade.

### The Filigree Clade (GG p14)

Obsessive perfectionist artisans; secret commissions for Inquisitors,
aristocrats, Rogue Traders, assassins. Infuriating to peers; protected by
their clients. **Campaign use:** the boutique source for any bespoke device
the plot needs; hooks involve a heretical Digital Weapon and a modified
Jokaero device loose in a hive.

### Izzatian Xenarites (GG p15)

Xenos-tech radicals from forge world Izzat (founded on a wrecked Aeldari
voidship). Lost their protector, rogue Inquisitor Eskender Thracius; now
procure alone, at risk. Hold the **Proxan Relic** — believed a casket of
machine spirits, actually an array of Aeldari Spirit Stones holding a dozen
souls being driven mad by their experiments; if the Aeldari learn, "they
would surely march to war." Continuing Thracius's plan to bait craftworlds
into war for salvage.
**Campaign use:** ties to Praefect Acquisitor Antae Rade (ex-Izzatian
Xenarite, now aboard the Ark under Lathe's appointment, GG p175); the Proxan
Relic is a ready-made catastrophe for a xenotech thread. For a Tech-Acq
patron: the Xenarites are the cautionary mirror of Vesper's "unlock powers
from the past" desire.

### Lexio Arcani (GG p16)

Data-diviners: "the classification of any data is heresy." Majority are Logi
(Explorators, Technoarcheologists, Lexmechanics, Transmechanics). Claim to
have predicted the Noctis Aeterna and the Genestealer cults from data
patterns; dismissed as fools by many, feared for the compromising data they
hold. Banished from the Archivium Technis (Triumvirate decree) and from the
Ark for "corrupting some of the Ark's sacred data stores." **Secretly
backed by Lathe Axis-438**, who shelters a clade aboard *Pierce the Veil*
(astrogation collation). Massed at listening posts near the **Ayzinith
Abyss** hunting the lost Ark **Rex Cogitatus**.
**Campaign use:** the Patron's secret sect (Hidden Adherent liability).
Hooks: "The Rusted Depths" (Ulysses Prompt believes a lost Archivium aboard
Recollections of Rust holds Rex Cogitatus's astrogation logs — cannot board
to get it) and "Data Corruption" (an Osmodia Beta datavault secretly holds
Archivium Technis construction plans).

### Macharia Vectorii (GG p17)

The heretek-hunters: enforce the Lex Mechanicus; founded by Obsidian
5-Penta during the Heresies. Led by the five **Pentactors**; signature
punishment "bionic abatement" (strip augmetics, melt them before the
condemned, cast the flesh to the void). GM truth: the Pentactors have become
**hardliners who now believe even Obsidian 5-Penta may be impure and intend
to prosecute their founder**.
**Campaign use:** the campaign's sword of Damocles — pressure on the
Xenarites, Psybernetica VI, the Patron's sect, and eventually on Lathe and
Obsidian both. Hook "No Atrocity Too Small" (GG p19): a novice on Augur
Array Decima-Theta accused of *improving his tools* — perfect early-mission
texture for how thin the line is.

### The Opaline Forge (GG p18)

Corpse-reclamators of Opal VII; recycle the bionics of the dead, believing
augmetic machine spirits retain experience and pass it to new bearers. Have
an enclave aboard the Ark. GM truth: their innermost circle, at **Obsidian
5-Penta's behest**, assembles the finest reclaimed bionics into **a perfect
flesh-free body for the Omnissiah to inhabit** — and Obsidian intends to
take that body for himself.
**Campaign use:** endgame-scale secret. Note the resonance with the Opaline
Vault aboard the Ark (gazetteer). Their creed (bionics carry ancestral
memory) is wonderful table-texture for every augmetic Vesper receives.

## Adeptus Terra relations (GG p7–8)

- **Adeptus Astartes:** respected, resented; they blast technoarcheological
  finds to deny them to enemies. All Chapters tithe gene-seed to the
  Mechanicus for purity testing. The **Minotaurs** Chapter — infamous
  suppressors of the Macharian Heresy — have a Strike Cruiser apparently
  shadowing *Recollections of Rust* (GG p8): unresolved, use as pressure.
- **Questor Mechanicus** (AdMech-sworn Knight houses): honoured allies.
  **Questor Imperialis** viewed with revulsion (own technicians outside the
  Cult). **House Lestor**, one of the Seven Swords, ruined by the heretic
  Titan *The Brazen Axe* during the Days of Blinding, is secretly
  considering swearing to the Mechanicus — first of the Seven Swords ever;
  if the other houses learn first, war (GG p8). The Triumvirate wants the
  deal.
- **Synod of Mars:** watching, waiting for the Triumvirate to stumble.

## Threats (GG p9, detailed p178–190)

- **Warpsmith Omneros:** invaded Eskutchax under the Noctis Aeterna;
  repulsed but survived; remnants hide underground sabotaging reclamation
  (scrapcode generators, daemon engines). Sees Eskutchax as the infection
  vector for every forge world. Agents active sector-wide; Triumvirate
  believes he is rebuilding for a second assault.
- **The Arkifane's Scions** (Vashtorr cult): heretek recruitment cult,
  "freedom from lex and doctrine," growing; **explicitly hunting the Ferric
  Locum's latest project** (FL-826). Formerly Plouton's own corrupted
  Tech-Adepts (GG p180).
- **Kirv's Salvagers** (Leagues of Votann): prospectors under Theyn Kirv
  Faenwrôght, scout ship *Ancestors' Ire*; hold a torn-off hull section of
  the Ark (Mission VI). Rival salvage claim engine; possible xenos contact.
- **Aeldari (Biel-Tan):** agents via hidden Webway gates; intersects the
  Xenarites' Proxan Relic time bomb.
- **Genestealer Cults:** hidden cells, hunted by the Inquisition; forge
  worlds are their ideal prizes.
- **The Vasarii** (GG p188): heretek martial cult of augmented warriors;
  hunted by Obsidian, **secretly fascinating to Lathe, who is in regular
  clandestine contact** (GG p190).

## Tech-heresy doctrine (GG p26–31) — the lines Vesper walks

Four pillars: Abominable Intelligence (absolute), gene-manipulation
(variable thresholds; reanimation absolutely forbidden; human-xenos splicing
worst), Warp technology (sanctioned only in Geller fields, Navigator
thrones, psykana), xenos technology (study for countermeasures tolerated;
use is crime; integration with Imperial patterns is worse). Root heresy:
**innovation** itself. Punishments: reassignment/exile → stripping of
augmetics and rank → execution with bionic abatement (GG p28).
Corruption exposure rules for Chaos/xenos tech: Minor 1 / Moderate 2 /
Major 4, resisted by Fortitude (physical), Discipline (spiritual), or Tech
(technological) (GG p29, PG p126–127).

Campaign relevance: Ryan wants "finding something and being able to use it."
The rules above are the friction that makes that fun and dangerous: *using*
a found artefact is often formally heretical, and the sanctioned path is
authenticate → contain → surrender. The Patron's creed ("curiosity without
containment is transgression") is the shield; the Lexio Arcani creed
("classification is heresy") is the temptation; the Vectorii are the
consequence.
