# The weave — metanarrative, GM-only

Last updated: 2026-08-14. This is the load-bearing document: how the five
books interlock into one world, and what Campaign 001 is actually about.
Rulings referenced here are formalized in [canon.md](canon.md).

## The campaign's question

Ryan's brief: a curious, never-heretical adept who loves *finding things and
unlocking the powers of the past*, with a ship, and Lady Lathe somewhere in
the sky. The setting's brief: **in the Mechanicus, discovery is the only
sanctioned progress, and it is one step from the only unforgivable sin.**
Innovation is heresy; archeotech is revelation; the same act of curiosity is
either, depending on containment, custody, and who is watching.

So the campaign is about **the custody of wonder**. Every act asks: you
found it, you can feel it wanting to wake — *whose is it?* The Patron says:
the Omnissiah's, through proper channels ("curiosity without containment is
transgression"). The Lexio Arcani say: everyone's ("classification of any
data is heresy"). Lathe says, privately: *mine, and I will decide what the
Imperium is ready for.* The Vectorii say: no one's, and burn the asker. Voll
says nothing, because what sleeps under Voll has not been asked in ten
thousand years.

## Timeline spine (see canon.md C1–C3)

Campaign 001 opens circa 012.M42, in the **closing years of Explorator
Rotation #46-DK-451** — i.e., *before* the published Explorator Macharia
campaign. Archmagos Plouton is the sitting Ferric Locum: distant, storied,
still secretly building FL-826-Alpha, his hand-picked Tech-Adepts (the
future Arkifane's Scions) quietly gathering materials across the sector.
The Ark's route is closing toward Port Optimar / Persepolis / Voll /
Macharia — which is why a Voll-based cell is well positioned.

- **Act 1 — Voll (the novice's world).** Investiture prelude; the
  Forge-Temple of the Tides (original, from the Aqua Regium seed, GG p39);
  Illicit Fabrications (VA); optionally The Eye of Voll (VA). Vesper's cell
  earns its reputation planetside while the Ark passes through the region.
- **Act 2 — Recollections of Rust (the wider church).** Vault work in the
  Explorator backlog; Malfunction Reports as connective tissue (Wayward
  Warriors = Lathe's entrance); Lexio Arcani favours begin arriving through
  Herazade's hidden channel; the Silent Trade thread from Voll resurfaces
  aboard (Auction Suite, *Opulent Tyrant*). The ship-ownership Endeavour
  runs through downtimes.
- **Act 3 — The collision.** The Voll thread (who buys unsanctioned
  wonders?) and the Patron thread (whose protection does her pipeline run
  under?) converge on Lathe's sphere just as the Vectorii/Zahn pressure
  peaks. Climax is a custody crisis over a specific find — ideally one
  Vesper personally awakened and loves.
- **Act 4 (optional next campaign) — Explorator Macharia.** The Plouton
  attack ends rotation #46 on schedule (canon C3): the workshop vents, the
  Locum dies, rotation #47 begins, and the Triumvirate must choose a new
  Ferric Locum — with unanimity. If Campaign 001's acts earned it, Herazade
  is on all three lists, and Ryan's cell inherits the published campaign
  from a position of deep personal history. Her Lexio Arcani secret then
  becomes the sword over the whole arc (Zahn's interrogation explicitly
  fishes for Hidden Adherent liabilities, GG p136).

During Acts 1–3, the future Scions may brush past the party namelessly
(canon C4): a too-brilliant Transmechanic (Kharo Thannek) consulting on
Voll's vox-grid; an Enginseer with not-quite-pattern bionics (Nikx Eta)
bidding against them for a find. Never name the organisation before the
attack; retroactive recognition is the payoff.

## Thread map

Each thread: fuse → how it burns → where it can end. Threads are fuel, not
rails; light them when play drifts near.

### T1 — The Drowned Choir (Voll's secret; canon C5)

The tectonically exposed forge-temple is one node of a **Dark Age
noospheric relay network** buried beneath Voll — the hives were unknowingly
built on its foundations ("ancient infrastructures dating back tens of
millennia," core p280). The acynadi are its bio-engineered maintenance
fauna, "parasites to a much larger organism" (VA p14) — the organism is the
network. The Unsea's chemistry is its self-cleaning medium (the Aqua Regium
chemical). To a noosphere-sighted adept the ruins sing — damaged, ancient,
patient. This is Vesper's personal mystery: the first machine that speaks
*back* in something like dialect. Slow reveal across all acts; endgame
scale. It is also physical evidence for the Xenarite heresy that the
noosphere "has always existed, akin to the Warp" and ancient Terran
technology merely accesses it (PG p129) — meaning the truth of Voll is
theologically explosive whoever learns it.

### T2 — The Silent Trade ladder (Voll → Ark → Lathe)

Voll's Hive Amlyr is a Silent Trade conduit (VA p10). Anything wondrous
that escapes containment on Voll gets brokered upward: noble house → sector
Infractionists (the Trackless Chain) → collectors like Lord Captain Rokhar
(*Opulent Tyrant*, GG p79) → and at the summit, quietly, Lathe (GG p57).
Vesper's cell keeps finding the ladder's lower rungs; each recovery makes a
rung-holder an enemy or an asset. The ladder is how act 3 reaches Lathe
without anyone planning it.

### T3 — The Patron's pipeline (Herazade / Lexio Arcani)

Tech-Acquisitor exotica arrive suspiciously well for a mid-rank curator:
her requisitions ride Lexio Arcani channels, which shelter under Lathe's
protection (GG p16). Sect favours arrive between missions (Hidden Adherent
texture): decrypt this, copy that vault, ask the Voll choir a question for
us. The sect's grail: the **Rex Cogitatus**, lost in the Ayzinith Abyss
(GG p62); their Ark-side wish: the lost Archivium aboard Recollections that
may hold its astrogation logs ("The Rusted Depths", GG p16). Herazade's
creed and her sect's creed cannot both be true forever — that is her
tragedy, and it should stay sympathetic.

### T4 — The watchers (Vectorii / Zahn)

Two independent eyes on the cell. The Macharia Vectorii watch Rade, the
Xenarites, and eventually anyone whose finds keep vanishing into vaults
("No Atrocity Too Small" shows how low their threshold is, GG p19). The
Pentactors' secret hardline turn (they now suspect even Obsidian, GG p17)
means NO ONE is safely orthodox — useful when the party thinks piety
protects them. Interrogator Zahn arrives with the Inquisition thread: she
befriends first, spies always, and her true Macharian target is **Obsidian
5-Penta himself** (GG p171). If the party's conduct is clean, she becomes
their strange half-ally against the Vectorii; if not, their leash.

### T5 — The ship (Ryan's wish, mechanised)

Session 1: a battered Arvus Lighter (Void Logistician), pilot included,
papers perpetually imperfect (Filed in Triplicate). Acts 1–2 downtimes:
Construct-or-Source Endeavour (PG p139) rebuilds a second, stricken Arvus
rite by rite — the cell's own hull, "ours because we healed it," with the
Repair Bay boon available later as a milestone (MRG p110 allows mid-campaign
grants). Act 2 sets the far prize in the sky: the derelict Endeavour-class
light cruiser drifting toward the flotilla's path (GG p71) — too big, too
haunted, claimable only by an institution. Act 3+/4: the salvage-claim
engine (registry vs proximity, GG p47) lets the cell's patron stake it; loans
of *Pierce the Veil* bridge the gap (GG p79/90). Endgame: crewing a true
voidship = becoming an institution (Locum-hood, Explorator posting), which
is the setting's honest answer to "owning" one. Crew accretion via Loyal
Companion (MRG p113): the pilot first, then a medicae hand, then — if act 4
happens — Ketheros.

### T6 — The Triumvirate cold war

Standing geometry for every Ark scene: Obsidian (dogma; hunts hereteks;
does not remember his own two heresies — Psybernetica VI, the Opaline
body), Lathe (liberated curiosity; Silent Trade, spy web, innovation),
Tolmet (unity by calculus; Sejanus's secret creature; targets Omneros).
Sejanus's engineering of Tolmet's seat (PG p13), Gnost's unresolved fall
(canon C6), and the Ferrous Whisper working to end all three (GG p13) keep
the top of the world unstable without requiring the party to touch it —
until they're senior enough that it touches them.

### T7 — The far prizes (reserve)

The **Heart of Rust** (GG p61): the Ark's dormant near-unlimited
manufactory — the biggest "found thing wanting to wake" in the setting;
never spend it casually. The **Rex Cogitatus** (T3's grail). The Minotaurs
shadow (GG p8). House Lestor's secret offer to the Mechanicus (GG p8).
These are decade-scale levers; one at most should ever be active.

## Tone dials

- Wonder first, dread second: every ruin gets one beautiful detail before
  its danger. The noosphere is a cathedral before it is a hazard.
- The Imperium is the horror; the machines mostly aren't. Machine spirits
  in this campaign are strange, wounded, occasionally kind. People do the
  atrocities.
- Mercy is available but priced (per Ryan's table style: "merciful to
  players" — the world is grim, the dice and GM are fair).
- Keep Lathe admirable for as long as possible. When the gap between her
  public and private faces finally shows, it should hurt.

## Multi-player readiness

Second-chair PCs slot in as: a Skitarii Ranger seconded from Incalcos-6
(Warrior/Penumbra), a Vigilite liaison or Administratum scribe from the
tithe office (Interlocutor/Savant), a Voidborn pilot-adept for the Arvus
(Penumbra/Savant), or later Ketheros rebuilt (Savant). Each pitch hooks an
existing thread (T4, T2, T5, and the act-4 inheritance respectively).
