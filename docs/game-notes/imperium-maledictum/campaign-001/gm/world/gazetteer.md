# Gazetteer — GM-only

Last updated: 2026-08-14. Places with campaign function. ☼ marks entries
with player-safe equivalents (core sector chapter, PG, or VA primer pages
3–14); everything else stays behind the screen until discovered.

## Voll and the Cytheris Subsector (Act 1 country)

- **Voll** ☼ (core p280; VA p7–14): hive world, Cytheris Subsector, ~100
  billion souls; bio-rich acidic slurry; sector's chemical/medicae
  monopoly; equidistant from Persepolis and Macharia, both dependent on
  it. Governor Jasper Castyx (Rogue Trader dynasty); Macharian Vigilites
  police. 36-hour day; Flowing/Tranquil/Unquiet tide-cycle synced to
  acynadi activity; unprotected atmosphere needs Fortitude (Endurance)
  every four hours. GM: T1 lives beneath it (canon C5).
- **Hive Rokarth** ☼: capital, ~30bn, spider-shaped above a web of
  mud-sluicing pipes; acid-flood scouring of mid-levels on Administratum
  timetables; Void Buttress is the orbital gateway (arrivals by Arvus).
  **Forge Fane Incalcos-6**: AdMech HQ on-world; keeps "the most complete
  account" of the acynadi; dispatcher of the Skitarii investigation in
  Illicit Fabrications; the cell's planetside anchor and the Forge-Temple
  mission's client.
- **Other hives** ☼: Dalal (House Nethecaré; verminvine); **Amlyr**
  (deposed House Kalveo, Castyx Regent; **Silent Trade conduit** — T2's
  bottom rung); Vishavar (House Taj; colossal-acynadi attacks; Knight House
  Vandensarg rotation); Sanksorak (House Vylathi; Ghasts of Voll penal
  legion; the Pale Lakes).
- **The wastes** ☼: the Unsea acid silt flats; the Stilt-fleet and
  Wastelanders (nomads — sympathetic faction in the Forge-Temple mission);
  washed-up ruins (canon C5); Ramm Kragon, burnt-out ex-Merchant-Fleet
  captain with a servo-skull and secrets (VA ~p44) — future ship-thread NPC.
- **Persepolis** ☼ (core p262): sector capital. **Macharia** ☼ (core
  p274): shrine world of Saint Macharius; the "Explosion Heard Across the
  World" archeotech lead (GG p38). **Port Optimar**: Imperial Fleet
  voidport; rotation #46 ends / #47 begins near it (GG p91).
- **Eskutchax** ☼ (core p270; PG p52; GG p8): premier forge world, still
  fighting Omneros's buried remnants and Esker's Bane scrapcode; Black
  Templars (Castellan Matias) and House Krast (Baron Vorgos) on-world;
  Esk-Relay-01 astropathic relay (GG Mission I). Plouton's homeworld and
  obsession. Act 4 country.
- **Belnshudha system**: quarantined; the *Final Turn of the Cog* salvage
  standoff between Hexas Panan (registry) and Osmodia Beta (proximity) —
  the salvage-law precedent (GG p47, canon-adjacent to T5).

## The Ark and its shadow (Act 2 country)

- **Recollections of Rust**: see cast.md and GG p60–79. Key sites: the
  Bridge (entombed captain), Augur Array Decima-Theta, Navigator Clade,
  Opaline Vault, Plasma Drives (palindromic Electro-Priest temple), Supply
  Vault Pavo-6 (House Pavix), Manufactorum Line Lambda-81, the Rustfall,
  Foundry of All Cogs, Auction Suite, moving black market, sealed
  Warp-scarred decks (Habitation Aelph-2; cogitator bank Theta-Kappa-77 of
  canon C14), **Acquisitor Vault Alpha-XIII** (Herazade's domain, GG p198),
  and the dormant **Heart of Rust** (GG p61; T7).
- **The flotilla** (GG p79): *Pierce the Veil* (Lathe's frigate; Lexio
  Arcani clade aboard), *Opulent Tyrant* (Rogue Trader collector — T2),
  *Corvus Chalybe* (Chartist alloys), *Aegis Macharia* (Zahn), *Justice of
  Persepolis* (Arbites+Vectorii), *Segmentatus Rex* (Iron Hands), penal
  frigate *Pillar of Redemption*, and the unexplained **Minotaurs Strike
  Cruiser** shadowing at range (GG p8).
- **The Jade Veil / Archivium Technis** (GG p19–21): hidden nebula void
  station holding the sector Mechanicus's duplicate knowledge; Cult
  Archivium stewardship; memory-chems for visitors; charted as listening
  post "AT-463"; the Paramount dataspool (Space Hulk Dantarus scrapcode)
  locked in sub-level five. The Ark crosses the Veil twice per rotation.
  Player knowledge of its existence is itself a plot event.

## The deep sector (Act 3+ country)

- **Ayzinith Abyss** (core p289; GG p62): Warp-anomalous region, Drimor
  Subsector approach; where the **Rex Cogitatus** vanished; Lexio Arcani
  listening posts ring it; the rogue signal that made the Ark's captain
  sever an augur array came from it (GG p71).
- **Arrian's Wrath** (core p293): ten trillion kilometres of shattered
  ships; salvage and pirates; Navigator-feared shoals. T5 hunting ground.
- **The Iron Archipelago** (core p289): chained mega-station hiding at
  least one space hulk; augur-blind; the sector's no-paperwork port
  (Xenarite Shadow Auction hook, GG p15). Where you buy a hull with no
  history, and pay for it in other ways.
- **Opal VII** (PG p53; GG p18): forge world of macrocemeteries; Opaline
  Forge seat; Ketheros's homeworld.
- **Tran'lo** (PG p55): forge world hunting the lost technology of the Vo;
  homeworld of Technoarcheologists and Invictus Acquisitors — Herazade's
  origin.
- **Dynne Alpha** (GG p101): Collegia Titanica forge world building the
  Warlord *Dynne Invictor*; Vectorii-dominated; Filigree Clade shrine
  hidden in Titanica Manufactorum 83-5. (Scions collected the Ciricrux
  module here pre-fall — cameo country, canon C4.)
- **Burza III** (GG p113): dying mining world; 34-year Iron Rain
  transuranium season; Houses Turri/Grigia; Arbitration of Iron.
- **Penance VI** (GG p84, p137): penal world, Secutor Lixis Vire's domain;
  Ketheros's prison.
- **Neptine X-5** (GG p120): generatorium void station near a black hole;
  Electro-Priest politics (Corpuscarii vs Fulgurite).
- **Terminus Iaysus** (PG p23; core p247): orbital port the Mechanicus
  built for Rogue Trader Ekaterina Praskovya Iaysus against promised
  technological discoveries — **she has not paid**. Lathe is arranging
  Explorator Crusades via the port. A debt the campaign can collect.
- **Other named forge worlds** (PG p50–55): Dannock III (initiates vanish
  for asking questions), Eskutchax, Hexas Panan (Biologis; empyric
  resonator hook GG p27), Levitys, Osmodia Beta (Logi data-vaults; Archivium
  construction plans in one, GG p16), Opal VII, Port Barbican (Data Delver
  origin), the *Assembly of Means* (drifting Goliath-class vessel at risk
  of becoming a space hulk; xenos-plasma reverse-engineering hook GG p28),
  Recollections of Rust (as origin), Tran'lo.
- **Izzat**: Xenarite founding site (wrecked Aeldari voidship); the Proxan
  Relic is wherever the GM needs it to be — currently canonically in
  Xenarite custody, location undisclosed (GG p15).
