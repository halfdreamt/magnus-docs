# Mission 1 cast — The Forge-Temple of the Tides

Compact briefs for the Voll mission's NPCs (session-1-investiture.md,
Mission 1). Each can be split into its own file if it recurs. Sourced
from VA pp. 12–14 (wastes, Stilt-fleet, guilds), core p280, and the GG
p39 seed; the people are Campaign-001 originals.

## Matriarch Ousel Brine-Aunt — Stilt-fleet family head

Family **Brine-Aunt**: ~a dozen souls on two multi-legged habunit
walkers (repurposed farm-stilters, VA p13), old-Voll blood, territory on
the Unsea's edge near the exposed ruin. Acynadi hunters and salvagers;
loosely allied to the **Mercator Carnem** (meat guild) for trade and
"protection" — a leash, not a friendship (VA p13: the guild presses
families into wars and sends mercenaries).
- **Voice:** she/her; sixty-ish, salt-scarred, rebreather-tanned; speaks
  slow, plain Low Gothic and does not fill silences; addresses adepts as
  "Cogboy/Coggirl" until they earn a name; laughs rarely and means it.
- **Wants:** the water-chemical — *clean water* is life on the flats;
  her diver back on his feet; to not be noticed by the hive.
- **Fears:** the guild learning what they found; the Mechanicus taking
  it and giving nothing back; the singing room (her diver's collapse
  frightened her more than she'll say).
- **Knows:** the ruin's under-layer (her diver saw stonework beneath the
  colonial plating — clue 1's Stilt-fleet route); the acynadi drink at
  the outflow (clue 2); the tides; where guild men are camped.
- **Stat-blocks:** her — Ganger (core p340) with Piloting (Civilian) 45
  and Navigation (Surface) 50; family — Dregs/Gangers, void-hardened,
  short-ranged weapons and gaffs. Resolve 2 for the family as a body.
- **Merciful ending:** the Brine-Aunts as Incalcos-6's sanctioned
  wardens of the site (clean water for them, custody for the
  Mechanicus). Contact if earned.

## Tesk Brine-Aunt — the diver

Ousel's grandson; the one who went into "the singing room" and came out
unconscious. Feedback shock, not injury: a Challenging Medicae (Human)
Test or a day's rest wakes him; a noosphere-sighted adept who *listens*
to him hears the Choir's cadence in his babble (clue 3's testimony
route). Awake, he is nineteen, frightened, and desperate to go back
because "it was singing *to me*." He is the mission's warning of what
grasping does to an unaugmented mind — and a mirror of Vesper at
fourteen.

## Vasco Truel — Mercator Lenimen factor

Chem-guild (VA p8: Mercator Lenimen = chems) buyer, on the flats with
six hirelings, bidding for "the water-maker." Answers, three rungs up, to
Hive Amlyr money (T2's Silent Trade ladder; canon C7).
- **Voice:** he/him; hive-sprawl smooth, guild-braid on his rebreather,
  calls everyone "friend," pays in scrip and promises; would rather buy
  than bleed but has bled before.
- **Wants:** a sample and the source location; failing that, silence
  bought or enforced.
- **Leverage on him:** he has no writ and the Vigilites never come out
  here (VA p8) — a quarantine writ from Incalcos-6 embarrasses him; a
  bribe now is cheap ("he sells his silence cheap NOW, expensively
  later"); a fight is his last resort. **Escapes with even a sample →
  thread T2 lights.**
- **Stat-blocks:** Truel — Merchant Trader (core p338); hirelings — 4
  Gangers + 2 Enforcers (core p340/p327), Resolve 1.

## Auditor-Adept Sabine Rho-11 — Incalcos-6 liaison

Forge Fane Incalcos-6's by-the-book Lexmechanic, dispatched late with
two Skitarii Rangers and the quarantine writ. She/her; ledger-minded,
suspicious of field improvisation, impressed by correct procedure and
*only* by correct procedure. Not an antagonist — an audit. If Vesper's
conduct is clean and his report leads with containment, she becomes an
Incalcos-6 Contact and the party's planetside AdMech anchor. Stat-block:
Lexmechanic (GG p203). Her Skitarii: Ranger stat-block (PG p150).

## The Choir node (not an NPC; not stattable)

See session-1-investiture.md, "The truth" and "the singing room." It is
never an enemy. It is damaged, patient, and — since Session 01 — the
GM knows Vesper approaches spirits by *attending to the pull within the
Rite* rather than grasping; the node should reward that method visibly
and punish grasping (as it did Tesk).
