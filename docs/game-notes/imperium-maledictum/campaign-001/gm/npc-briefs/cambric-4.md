# Overseer Cambric-4

Mid-rank reclamation overseer, *Recollections of Rust*. Campaign-001
original (flashback debut, Session 01).

## Voice and manner

Flat vox-grille monotone; clipped, procedural; quantifies everything;
despises imprecision and wasted shift-time; secretly weary. Stamp-hand
hisses — a tired tic when thinking. Values face above almost everything;
responds well to ladders he can climb down, and never forgets who built
the ladder or who made him need one.

## Knows / wants / fears

- Knows: reclamation writs, deck-level salvage procedure, decades of the
  Ark's lower-deck gossip. Signed the original Theta-Kappa-77 survey.
- Wants: quota met, record clean, no surprises.
- Fears: an overturned survey discovered by a superior; being made to look
  imprecise.
- Private: unit 41 gave him a half-second of unease during the survey; he
  logged it as capacitor bleed and chose to forget. Will never admit it.

## State

- [Session 01, flashback, ~11 years ago] Initiate Vesper blocked the
  release of unit Theta-Kappa-77-KO ("singing wrong"), crediting
  Cambric's stamp with the reactivation. Cambric took the ladder:
  suspended unit 41 pending Tier-3 auscultation per code 9-Theta — logged
  under *Vesper's* designation, not his own. Internal relief; his survey
  stands. He has not forgiven the correction, precisely because it saved
  him.
- [Present day] Still working reclamation; now outranked in practice by
  the boy he once stamped past. Disposition toward Vesper: grudging,
  correct, watchful.
