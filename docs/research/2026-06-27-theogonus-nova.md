# Theogonus Nova

## Saturn above the Abyss — the Gardenhead

Saturn is prior to everything. She exists above the Abyss, before the world and
outside it. She alone holds the multi-world nature of reality in view: each world,
with its lesser gods, is a leaf on a tree in her garden. She is the **Gardenhead**
— *"the Gardenhead knows my name."* The most primal deity sees you and knows you;
the great mother does not forget her children. She will also eat them all, as the
ground eats them, or the sky — it makes no difference which. Like her rings, she
surrounds us in all time and all space.

She creates without words, without even personifying herself. She makes the world,
and she makes the people in it, and she says nothing. She is a dark, silent mother.
Because she neither speaks nor shows herself, the children may not recognize her at
all. They are uncertain of their own origin, so they invent it — narratives,
stories. The firstborn may claim to have made the rest, and only the firstborn
knows the truth: that it, too, simply appeared, out of thin air, or fire, or water,
or earth, or spirit, exactly as the others did. The origin myths the people tell
are false. Saturn is the silent truth beneath them.

Saturn is the **first entity of the pantheon** — present as a deity from the start,
though she never personifies herself *to her children*, who therefore cannot
recognize her. She sets the whole thing in motion and, in effect, invites the other
gods into her world. She **challenges** her children; she curses them only to watch
them grow. She wants Venus to discover gathering and to manage birthing — the curse
is the provocation that calls the blessing, and the god who answers it, into being.

## The technologies are coins

Each foundational technology is a **coin paid in full, not in sides.** A coin has a
curse-face and a blessing-face, and you cannot buy one without the other.

- **Why bundle.** Thematically, curse and blessing are two sides of one thing — to
  need is also to be able to meet the need; each coin is a **balanced perturbation
  that contains its own cancellation.** Mechanically, that balance is what keeps the
  simulation *bounded*: a coin mostly changes the *quality* of the computation, not
  its *quantity*, so the world never becomes inherently unperformative — performance
  is tuned per-technology, and only a few techs ever need reining in. The unbalanced
  case is exactly what bundling forbids: **birth without death** would grow population
  without limit (unbounded entities, unbounded CPU), so it must never be reachable.
- **A coin is a single *toggle*, not a single *concept*.** The two faces stay
  distinct ideas; they are only fused at the switch. And we do **not** insist that
  every coin be one-curse/one-blessing — that framing is the *thematic* spine of the
  early tiers, not a structural law on every technology to come.

## Agency — Saturn sends; the others answer

Who controls a technology is its own axis, and it is the spine of play. **Saturn's
coins are *sent*, not chosen** — hunger falls, death falls, whether or not anyone is
ready. The player never "turns on" a curse, and so never faces a lose/lose; Saturn
does it to them. All the agency lives in the *response*: which child to ascend into
the newly opened role, and how that god then administers.

A coin can be Saturn-*sent* and still carry another god's controllable levers inside
it — like a Magic card that is one color but has another in its text box. Ownership is
about *who decides*, not which domains appear. So the early game is **the community
versus Saturn**: she throws curses across all of time, and they answer by building a
pantheon. The line is clean: **Saturn sends coins 1–2 (hunger, death); the community *chooses*
everything from coin 3 on (Leadership, Family, …).** This gives two flavors of agency.
For a *sent* coin you choose only the **response** — whom to ascend, or starve. For a
*chosen* coin you choose whether to **adopt** the institution at all: Leadership and
Family are optional improvements with their own costs, not survival-mandatory like
answering hunger. Agency — who is able to do what — is the concept to track from here;
the divine levers are taking shape now, and mortal levers come later.

## The foundational sequence

| # | Coin | Owner | What enters |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | **The World and its People** | Saturn | time + space, borne; the soul pool — identity; the golden age, deathless |
| 1 | **Hunger and Toil** | Saturn (hunger) · Venus (toil) | the need to consume + the labor to meet it; **mortality as starvation** (death by lack); Venus ascends, with a lever on gather yield |
| 2 | **Death and Birth** | Saturn (death) · Venus (birth) | Saturn imposes **old-age death** (aging out of labor; children too young to labor) on all the living; Venus answers with **birth** and the **birth-control** lever |
| 3 | **Leadership and Community** | Zeus | *chosen, not sent.* Dependence (hunger + death) forces pooling; the **leader** (chosen by **prestige**) holds the pool — the first drain — and drifts to tyranny (neglect of the needy) unless Zeus steers him |
| 4 | **Marriage and Family** | Zeus · Venus | *chosen; independent of coin 3.* Zeus binds Venus's mate-selection; **family** brings continuity (inheritance, paternity) but privatizes the pool and makes **prestige heritable** → dynastic tyranny |
| 5+ | Crowding · Agriculture · migration | — | further social order (downstream — see below) |

Coins **1 and 2 have no forced order** with each other — Hunger & Toil and Death &
Birth are independent — but **both must precede coin 3**: Leadership/Community exists
to answer a dependence that hunger and death only create together (below).
**Marriage and Family does *not* require Leadership**, nor the reverse — both are
independent, *chosen* coins. But family's **curse** only bites once Community exists:
privatization needs a pool to fragment, so family alone (pre-community, where everyone
is already private) is nearly inert. Independent toggles; the harm is an interaction,
not a prerequisite.

**0 — The World and its People (Saturn).** Bundled deliberately: a world with no
people is pointless in a people-centred simulation, so the substrate and the souls
arrive on one coin. Time and space, the world created and *borne* — Saturn is its
true bearer. The soul pool is timeless and nearly static: person-entities that
exist and carry an identity, being and vibing, with no hunger, no death, no birth
and **no communities** — just people and the stories they tell about where they came
from. This alone, with the later coins off, is **Saturn's golden age**. Counted among
the technologies it is the **0th** — a deliberate inversion, since the World is
normally the *last* card of the tarot, and here it comes first.

(Retire **"the bearing world"** entirely. The earth does not *bear* the people; when
they stray into the mud the worldly fruit only *gets them by*, so imperfectly that
it wears them into dust. Saturn bears; the land gets you by. The land's yielding
belongs to Venus, at the next coin.)

**1 — Hunger and Toil (Saturn · Venus).** Hunger is the curse-face: to have strayed
into the world is to need to consume, and to fail is to starve. This is Saturn's,
and with it **mortality enters the world for the first time, as starvation** — death
by lack, before any clock on the body. Toil is the blessing-face: one of Saturn's
children finds the food and shares it, and in that act of mercy she is lifted out of
the soul pool into the **first non-Saturn godhood — Venus**, who draws sustenance
from the earth and gives it to the others.

Toil is also the introduction of **labor** itself: people become bound to an
activity, and since there is no specialization before community, there is exactly one
role — **gather** (perhaps hunt). The moment hunger arrives, everyone has a job for
the first time. And the child who ascends to Venus is, by ascending, made
**immortal** — immune to starvation and senescence, killable only under special,
yet-to-be-determined circumstances.

**2 — Death and Birth (Saturn · Venus).** Death is the curse-face, and Saturn
*sends* it: senescence, a clock on the body, **applied to everyone already alive** the
moment the coin lands — the deathless long-lived included. Death also introduces
**aging**: people age *out* of the labor pool, and birth brings **children** too young
to labor. Birth is the blessing-face — Venus answers with reproduction, more people to
stand against the loss — and it carries her second lever: **birth control**, framed as
*deciding who to give it to* (managing who is born, not a blunt fertility dial).
Unmanaged, gathering starves a tract by overharvest; unmanaged, birth starves it by
overpopulation — Venus holds both sides of the food-and-mouths balance. Saturn is
spiteful — and yet proud of her daughter.

**3 — Leadership and Community (Zeus · Venus).** Dependence is the trigger, and it is
*emergent* — it appears only once hunger and death **both** exist. An elder aged out of
labor, or a child too young for it, is only at risk when food must be *labored* for
(hunger) and bodies *age* (death); either curse alone is harmless. The two **compound**
into a need the golden age never had: to be cared for.

Pre-community there is no sharing — each person gathers for themselves and their own,
and surplus sits useless in private hands while the needy starve beside it (the player
can *see* it: full inventories, no way to give). **Community** is the gift: pool the
surplus and the needy are easily fed. But a pool must be counted, held, and handed
out — a full-time role that *drains* rather than adds: the **leader**, the first such
role. And the need for a leader is itself a curse, like hunger and death — the trap
(below).

**Chosen, not sent.** Hunger and death Saturn *sends*; Leadership/Community is the
community's own *answer* to the dependence those curses create — **offered, not
inflicted**, the first coin with mortal agency. A world may decline it and simply let
its dependents die.

**4 — Marriage and Family (Zeus · Venus).** The first coin that is not about Saturn at
all: where the earlier coins were the community *versus Saturn*, this one is **Zeus
reaching into Venus's domain** — the pantheon turning on itself (see *Marriage*,
below). **Family** is the gift of **continuity** — inheritance, genetics, paternity:
the only lever by which anything persists between individuals across generations. It is
also **private property** — the community's pooled surplus fragments into **family
stockpiles** *instead*. That fragmentation is the curse: the needy lose the
community-wide safety net (a poor family starves beside a rich one) and, worse,
inheritable **prestige** makes tyranny **hereditary** — a leader's drift now passes to
his bloodline, so succession is gated by blood and **Zeus's successor lever is
undermined**. A bad leader becomes a bad *dynasty*. Its curse is **chosen, not sent**:
a world may run without paternity or family, at the cost of all continuity. It is
**independent of Leadership** (neither requires the other), but its curse only
*manifests* alongside Community and a leader's prestige — there must be a pool to
privatize and a standing to inherit.

## Two curses, both Saturnic (the thematic spine)

Read thematically, the early coins are a ladder of curse answered by blessing, and
the deities sort cleanly across it — *both deaths are Saturn's,* **starvation** and
**old age** alike, even though they ride on coins we associate with Venus's gifts.
Saturn curses (the world, and the reclaiming of it); Venus blesses (food against
hunger, birth against death), her every gift an answer to her mother. The mother
bears and curses; the daughter answers; the mother is proud. *(Spine, not law — a
later coin need not be shaped this way.)*

## Administration — Venus's lever on gather yield

Hunger and Toil opens the first divine vacuum, and what fills it is an
**administrator**. Gathering is the first **administrable technology**: the need is
born the instant labor exists, so Venus does not wait on a later tier to administer —
her domain *is* the management of this first labor.

The concrete form: **Venus holds a lever on gather-labor yield that requires
administration.** Mortals lack the information the work needs — a tract's carrying
capacity, whether it is being overharvested — and Venus is the single person who can
see it and direct them (or not). Left unadministered, gathering overharvests, the
tract's capacity falls, and yield with it. Her lever is keeping the harvest
*sustainable*; it is naturally a feedback loop (harvest pressure against regrowth),
and the micromanagement it implies — making sure plots aren't overharvested — is the
texture of the role.

These administration tasks should be **automatable** but **fun to play** for anyone
who wants to take Venus's part; for now they're abstracted. This sets a pattern worth
holding: **each labor type has a domain and a technology that can be administered** —
though not every technology need have an administrative lever. Venus's eventual path
to **agriculture** likely runs through persistent labor administration.

**Open — what unadministered labor costs.** Not yet decided, and the choice sets how
lethal the pre-Venus gap is: *degraded yield* (gathering, but inefficient/declining),
*zero yield* (without knowing what's edible, foraging nets nothing), or *harm*
(poison, bad preparation — labor that hurts). These aren't mutually exclusive.

## Leadership's trap — the tyranny dynamic and Zeus's lever

The divine levers sit on different axes and never collide. **Venus owns production** —
gather yield, who is born. **Zeus owns the leader.** The structure is nested, each
layer a little system with a narrow output: gathering takes labor and yields **food**;
**leadership is the labor whose product is the organization of other labor** — the
leader's "what do I spend my time and resources on?" resolves into the community's
"what do *we* spend ours on?", which is **labor allocation**. Venus makes food; the
leader makes order. So Zeus does not assign labor himself — the *leader* does; **Zeus
administers the leader**, micromanaging where the leader spends time and resources, the
way Venus micromanages the gatherers.

The trap is that **leadership corrupts its holder.** For this, personality becomes
**dynamic** — no longer fixed at birth but in flux across a life, giving each person an
arc shaped by circumstance. Holding power is a strong such circumstance: it makes a
person **less grounded**, and ungroundedness is what tips generosity toward greed. So
a leader left alone **drifts toward tyranny**. Tyranny manifests first as **neglect**,
not theft: before families there is no kin to hoard *for*, so the un-grounded leader
simply stops doing the welfare distribution that justified the role — the dependents
fall through the cracks, the needy starve beside a surplus no one bothered to share.
The question is *who doesn't get fed*. (With **Family** it escalates: now there is kin,
so neglect sharpens into favoritism — feed mine, neglect yours — and, with heritable
prestige, turns dynastic.) Because the *role* drives this, not a rare trait, the drift
is *reliable* — every leader trends this way, which is what makes anti-tyranny
administration necessary rather than occasional. The gift carries its own poison.

**Zeus's lever** is therefore not the old quick-swap of leaders. It is, first, to
**steer a sitting leader away from tyranny** (mechanism TBD), and second, when one
must be replaced, to **choose a successor unlikely to drift**. Unadministered
leadership rots into tyranny the way unadministered gathering rots into overharvest —
the same shape as Venus's domain, which is what should make it satisfying.

**Who becomes leader — prestige.** Today the leader is set by a *family's* authority
value; here the disparity lives in the leader's *personality*, so selection needs a new
currency: **prestige**, a per-*individual* standing. The leader is the high-prestige
person, and **Zeus does not appoint directly — he tips the selection** toward
non-drifters and steers the one in office. Prestige is individual by default, so it
dies with its holder and Zeus picks fresh — until **Family makes it heritable**, when
high prestige passes down the bloodline and selection ossifies into dynasty. That is
family's curse, stated mechanically.

**Open / to flesh out:** **what generates an individual's prestige** (contribution —
surplus gathered, dependents cared for? personality? age?) — the live question, since
both selection and family's curse hang on it; what drives the corruption (time in
power? size of the pool?); which personality axis carries "groundedness" (the existing
temperament has a rootedness↔drive axis and a spirit/integration value); and how Zeus
"steers" concretely.

## Marriage — the first inter-deity lever (Zeus binds Venus)

Every lever so far has been a god upon a curse or a god upon mortals. **Marriage is the
first god upon a *god*.** Who mates with whom was Venus's alone, part of her
reproduction domain. **Marriage is Zeus laying a heavy modifier on it** — marrying off
and marrying into the family stock to manage resources, genetics, and prestige into the
future. It is **not finally binding** (Venus can still play her games), but it makes
them far harder, which is why she **resents** it. This is the pantheon's first internal
conflict — the beginning of "the six in struggle."

Family is a **structure, not a labor role** — there is no gather-style per-tick output;
what it "produces" is lineage and **heritable prestige**. (A "head of household" role,
the family-level echo of the leader, is possible but not adopted.) Prestige itself is
the individual leader-selection standing from *Leadership's trap* above; family's part
is only to make it **inheritable**.

## Seeding the world — one tract, a pantheon's worth of souls

The world begins as **a single tract**, with **all the people created in one space**
— unlike the current engine, which scatters several communities at generation. The
starting population is **close to the number needed to fill the initial pantheon**:
the first people *are* the gods-to-be. The pantheon is filled from these founders,
emergently, as each technology that needs a divine role comes online — and (a
deterministic sim) fixed for a given seed and config. *How* a founder comes to fill
a role — by what trigger, by what selection — is **not yet worked out**, and isn't
documented here.

**Saturn is not among them** — she is prior, the creator, never a mortal. The named
functions are **Venus** (ecology), **Mercury** (the knowledge-sharer), and **Jupiter**
(rule); **Mars** is in the pantheon with a role still to come, and **Sol and Luna** are
looser. The roster is not locked.

**Sol — the rejected stone — is the founder of all mortals.** The founder left
without a domain role becomes the literal progenitor: he stays fertile *because*
unchosen and fathers the mortal line, while the role-bearing gods do not reproduce.
"The stone the builder refused becomes the headstone." So **deity siring/birthing is
disallowed** for the role-bearing gods; Sol is the fertile exception, and the
**demigod lines** that the *other* gods' offspring would imply are **not modeled
yet**.

## Downstream — later coins

Beyond Marriage and Family: **migration / founding** (a hard prereq on Leadership —
the game is one tract until a leader exists), **crowding**, and **agriculture**
(Venus's, likely reached through persistent labor administration). These read as **net
positives** — not every coin needs a downside, and that is fine.

**New families** are created at pairing: instead of marrying into an existing family,
there is a chance a *new* one is founded, weighted by personality — which maps onto the
engine's existing **fission** logic, not new machinery.

## Divergence from the 06/17 refactor

This supersedes [the technology refactor](2026-06-17-technology-refactor.md) on
sequence and structure:

- **06/17 bundled birth, both deaths, and consumption into one tier** (Birth &
  Death), with Food Yield as a *later* tier. **Now mortality arrives in two stages:**
  starvation rides **Hunger & Toil** (an earlier coin, with toil/food fused in), and
  senescence rides **Death & Birth** (a later coin) — order-free with Hunger & Toil,
  but both precede Leadership.
- **06/17 listed community/grouping under the People tier.** **Now there are no
  communities until Leadership** — the soul pool is identity only.
- **New:** the coin/bundle principle (pay in full coins for boundedness); the **agency
  axis** resolved — Saturn *sends* coins 1–2, the community *chooses* coins 3+;
  single-tract pantheon-sized seeding; deity sterility with **Sol the mortal
  progenitor**; dependence as an **emergent** product of hunger + death; a reworked
  **Leadership** (nested levers — leader allocates labor, Zeus administers the leader;
  tyranny = **neglect** of the needy; selection by **prestige**, not family authority,
  Zeus tipping it); and **Marriage and Family** as the first **inter-deity** coin (Zeus
  binding Venus) — independent of Leadership, its curse making prestige heritable
  (dynastic tyranny).
