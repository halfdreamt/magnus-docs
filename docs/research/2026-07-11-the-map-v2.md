# The Map v2 — one world, drawn to be framed

**Date:** 2026-07-11
**Status:** Design (Luna-authored; follows Ryan's verdict on the first pass: "a great
first pass... a bit rough as an end product. The changes weren't as drastic as I had
hoped... it's too low resolution. And not very styled.")
**Related:** 2026-07-11-the-map.md (the archetypal derivation — its element table is
still the requirements), branch `map-v2` (based on `map-geography` + master merged).

## The diagnosis

Two separate uglinesses, two separate fixes:

1. **Structural.** The world is N independent 8×8 region grids — three postage
   stamps, each with its own private coastline, its own private range. No world
   silhouette exists to draw. The masters' maps are ONE landmass whose shape you
   remember; ours is a contact sheet. No resolution increase inside the tiling
   fixes this.
2. **Cartographic.** 64 cells per region rendered as flat fills in a 260px drawer,
   system-font labels, no relief, no smoothing — grid confetti at any zoom.

## The decision: one continuous world grid

Evaluated three candidates against the binding rules (per-build determinism,
ordered iteration, RNG-rides-raw-ids, value components) and the consumer catalog
(every engine read/write of SpaceState/grid/GridPosition/path_cells was swept):

- **Finer per-region grids (32×32/region)** — cheapest migration, but keeps the
  postage stamps: no world silhouette, no range-as-border-between-cultures, no
  cross-region journey on the map. Rejected: it spends the whole budget without
  touching ugliness #1.
- **Voronoi + Lloyd (Azgaar-style) tract layer** — the organic look, but every
  consumer addresses space as an integer (x,y) lattice: plot_grids key arithmetic
  (`y*W+x`) in four places, ring/grid scans in Fission/Beasts/Wizards, in_bounds
  math, export decode. Irregular cells rewrite all of it AND make neighbor
  iteration order a fresh determinism hazard. Rejected: the organic look is
  obtainable at render time (marching squares + Chaikin over cell data — smoothing
  is where Azgaar's own coastlines come from), so the sim layer doesn't need to
  pay for it.
- **One continuous world grid** (~96×64 tracts for 3 regions, config-scaled),
  regions as contiguous partitions grown from seeded capitals by terrain-aware
  multi-source Dijkstra — so region BORDERS fall along ranges and rivers
  (geography as politics, for free). **Chosen.**

The hierarchy survives intact: **region** (partition of the world grid) → **area**
(flood-fill within the region, retuned count) → **tract** (world grid cell) →
**plot** (per-tract grid, unchanged). GridPosition becomes world coords — same
component, same type, no registration shift.

## What the consumer sweep proved (and what it licenses)

- `tract_dist` (the all-pairs N² matrix — the scaling wall at fine resolution:
  ~33MB/region at 64×64) has exactly TWO consumers, both community↔community:
  marriage reach (MarriageSystem) and route adjacency (RoadsSystem). **Replaced by
  per-community distance fields**: one Dijkstra field (N uint16) computed at the
  community's founding (creation-time seeding — the sanctioned write), stored on
  SpaceState in an id-ordered map. O(1) lookups survive; memory is
  communities×N ≈ 50×6144×2B ≈ 600KB; the geography-off Euclidean fallback stays.
- Everything else (Mars raids, beasts, wizards, fission scoring) is raw Euclidean
  on GridPosition — terrain-blind today, terrain-blind after (unchanged v1; noted
  seam). World coords make their cross-region distances *meaningful* instead of
  undefined.
- Routes/marriage/Mars pairing was same-region-only — an artifact of disconnected
  space, not a design. On a continuous world the gate becomes DISTANCE (which the
  halfsat terms already implement). Pairing goes world-wide; the region remains
  the naming/administrative layer. This is what makes the chokepoint composition
  world-real: the pass carries THE road between two region-cultures.
- Config knobs denominated in cells (predation 4, wilderness 6, dragon 14, tower
  6/10/8, adjacency 6.0, marriage 5.0) rescale by the linear refinement (~×3.5).
  AreaGenerator's `cells/6` area count retunes to ~12–16 areas/region (the
  one-community-per-area cap becomes the region's room to grow — the sanctioned
  population scale-up, reported honestly in the perf row).

## Terrain synthesis v2 (Saturn's creation act, world-scale)

Order: (1) domain-warped fBm elevation with seeded continent falloff — an
irregular landmass with sea on the map's edges, drowned to land_fraction by
quantile; (2) mountain ranges as ridge WALKS between seeded endpoints (momentum
walk, not staircase), elevation raised along the ridge, passes punched at the
lowest saddles; (3) region partition (capitals by farthest-point on land,
growth by terrain-aware Dijkstra — walls and water are expensive, so borders hug
them); (4) rivers from ridge sources by descent WITH RECORDED COURSE (ordered
polylines, exported for drawing); (5) biome fields by elevation/moisture
quantiles, feature_scale widened so forests are provinces, not confetti;
(6) base_fertility from terrain (unchanged table); (7) GeoFeatures named
world-level, only major components (labels must not spam); (8) wilderness by
multi-source BFS from founding communities; (9) per-community distance fields.

Every step a pure function of (world_seed, config); ordered iteration throughout;
no new component registrations (all state rides SpaceState — the existing
singleton).

## Export v2

Terrain becomes ONE world block, columnar (`width, height` + parallel arrays:
terrain codes, region_id, flags bitfield [pass|river|bank], wilderness, fertility,
elevation) — smaller JSON, one parse. Rivers as ordered courses. Routes gain a
`path` polyline (A* over the same cost field at export time — roads bend through
passes on the map). geo_features world-level. grid_x/grid_y stay the same field
names, now world coords. plot_grids/areas same shapes, world coords. All folded
into the checksum as before.

## Cartography v2 (the frame test)

The drawer-sized scrolling canvas becomes a real map surface: fit-to-viewport
camera with three zooms (world → region → tract), cached static layers
(offscreen canvases: relief, water, features, labels — redrawn only on
data/zoom change), dynamic overlay (towns/routes/pale/beasts) per tick.

Layer stack, bottom to top: parchment ground (procedural, dark-theme);
sea with depth shading + coast outline via **marching squares over the sea mask,
Chaikin-smoothed**, double-stroked with a water-glow band; terrain fills as
painterly washes tinted by elevation hillshade (light from NW); rivers as
Chaikin-smoothed polylines widening downstream; **hachured ranges** — peak
strokes with shaded SE flanks along the ridge, foothill dashes; forests as
jittered tree-glyph clusters (seeded, deterministic); swamp tufts, desert dune
strokes; the danger pale as a desaturating wash; region borders as faint
dot-dash with region names in spaced capitals; roads as dashed umber paths along
exported route polylines; site glyphs redrawn as a proper glyph set (town dot →
walled town by population, ruin, tower, shrine, cave mouth, dragon lair, beast
dens); GeoFeature labels in the cartographic hand (serif italics, letter-spaced
smallcaps for seas/regions, curved along river courses), greedy collision
avoidance; a compass rose and scale bar; neatline frame. Canvas, vanilla JS,
no frameworks, no font downloads (system serif stack, styled).

Fixes that ride along: venue glyphs were dead (no grid pos in export — placed at
their community now), shrines/wolf/lion/bear positions exported but never drawn.

## Verification (playbook full table + slice-specific)

Build + validate-config + lint clean; determinism ×2 (two seeds); geography A/B
(off = flat-terrain world on the new grid, machinery inert); **the chokepoint
composition re-proven world-scale** — routes concentrate at passes (cross-wall
routes thread the pass tracts; named), stance diverges across the wall vs along
it; settlement sanity (no drowned/peak towns); golden re-record (trajectories
move — greenfield); perf row honest (worldgen cost + season-loop delta + table
count; population growth reported); Playwright screenshots at 3 zooms — the
glance test.
