# New Tenants — content for the shapes we built

**Date:** 2026-07-12
**Status:** Design (Luna-authored on the night shift; Ryan's brief: "Looking at the
shapes of what we've added, the capability for types of content, and adding a
significant more. Get creative. Vampires, machines, fleshing out magic
significantly, fleshing out combat significantly… and a lot of other stuff we can
think of.")
**Related:** the-wide-world.md (Beings/Sites/discovery-table/Works — the shapes),
blooded-lines.md, the-end-of-the-world.md (eras/hands/stamps — assumed landed),
the-weave.md (edges these tenants will inherit)

## The method

The wide-world built shapes; the End built deep time. A new tenant is strong
exactly when it rents *several* rooms at once: a Being with a Site, made by a
coin, carrying Works, leaving residue an era can inherit. Every tenant below is
scored against that — nothing arrives that the existing frameworks can't hold.

## N1 — Vampires (the undying tenant)

E3 handed us the design on a plate: *undead that outlive their binder* is the one
missing mechanic between us and a true Age-of-Crows death — and it is also,
precisely, a vampire. A vampire is a person the record already knew, risen
**self-bound** (no necromancer's leash): they keep their name, their face, their
grudges — and stop aging.

- **Origin, diegetic:** rare dark outcomes of the existing machinery — a
  necromancer's strike gone deeper, a cursed death, the dark gift *given* (a
  vampire makes another; the dark mirror of Blooded Lines — a line that spreads
  by bite, not birth, with its own potency decay).
- **The feeding loop:** predation on persons (the Beast idiom, but the predator
  has a name and a history with its prey's family); a fed vampire is subtle, a
  starved one is a rampage — grievance and hunts follow naturally.
- **The lair:** a Site (ruin/cave/tower claimed — the third apex tenant beside
  the dragon and the exile); or, darker, *residence in society* — the elder of
  the town who has not aged in sixty years, until someone counts.
- **Deep time is the payoff:** a vampire crosses turnings. The ancient in the
  era-2 ruin who *remembers the First Age firsthand* — the only living memory of
  a dead people. And with self-bound undead, the dead can finally outnumber the
  living at a world's death: **the Age of Crows becomes landable** (close E3's
  seam as part of this wave).
- **Containment:** hunts/expeditions extend (the stake party); the Keeper's ward
  arts get their apex target.

## N2 — Arms & Scars (combat fleshed out, sim-side)

Combat today is aggregate: power sums, weighted casualties. The depth wanted is
*individual consequence* — every clause riding what exists:

- **Works go to war.** A carried blade-Work raises its bearer's combat power;
  custody meets combat. Named weapons earn their legends mechanically ("Ferrum's
  bearer has repelled three raids") — and are looted when their bearer falls
  (S1's raid-loot edge, completed from the combat side).
- **Wounds persist.** Beyond kill/wound: scars and maiming on HealthState — the
  lamed Blade turns Keeper, the scarred survivor carries the raid for life; the
  chronicle already loves this register.
- **Champions and duels.** Named single combat at the story joints: a raid's
  fate turned by the two named champions who met at the gate; a leader's
  challenge; the Blade who faces the dragon alone. Small mechanic (a seeded duel
  resolution off combat power + works + scars), enormous chronicle yield.
- **Veterancy.** Survived battles grow a fighter's power/prestige — the old wolf
  of six raids is a fact of the roster, and the raider culture's dread champion
  emerges without being authored.

## N3 — The Grimoire (magic fleshed out)

Magic today is world-level (coins) and archetypal (the discovery table). The
missing middle is magic as *material culture*:

- **Spells are Works whose medium is text.** An adept at the peak of a
  discovery writes the grimoire — a Work with maker/date/place provenance,
  inherited, carried, stolen, burned, *left in ruins*. The stamp machinery (E6)
  does the rest: **a grimoire whose coin is not in the current hand is a lost
  spell** — literally Ryan's "a spell similarly impossible, or lost, forgotten,"
  now findable in a dungeon with a custody chain.
- **Teaching chains.** Arts pass person-to-person (who taught whom is
  provenance for knowledge — the lineage of a craft, the last living practitioner
  a real, queryable person whose death extinguishes an art in-era).
- **Rituals.** Community-scale castings at shrines/towers — the visible face of
  the counter-arts (the warding of a town, the laying of a plague) and of the
  dark ones (the rite that calls the horde). Rituals are events with named
  celebrants; the scene query finds a rite in progress.

## N4 — The Made (machines)

Constructs: **Works that act**. A construct is forged like any Work (maker, date,
place — provenance intact) under a machine-craft coin category (E6's registry
growth), and then it *stands up*: a Being whose control law is service.

- **Binding:** serves its maker, then the maker's line or community — and, in
  the night's running theme, **outlives them**. A masterless construct wanders,
  or stands sentinel over a dead town for three hundred years.
- **Dormancy is the era payoff:** when the machine-craft coin leaves the hand,
  constructs wind down where they stand — *dormant in the ruins of the Age of
  Machines*, inert Works in a hoard until… found. Waking one is a session, not a
  mechanic (the DM's spellbook gets a `wake` cast; the sim keeps them sleeping).
- **The construct is the anti-vampire:** both cross eras; one remembers and
  hungers, the other waits and serves. A world with both has its two ancient
  voices.

## N5 — The Salt Road (the creative extra: boats)

The map already made islands and left them waiting: "offshore isles stay
unsettled until the world has boats — a feature wearing a seam's name."
A seafaring coin pays that debt: fishing (a Venus food channel with its own
famine texture), sea routes (the route machinery over water edges), **island
settlement** (fission across the strait — island cultures with divergent stance,
the ASOIAF element the map doc extracted), and ships as community Works (built,
named, wrecked — a wreck is a site). Sea beasts tier into the Being registry;
the drowned town enters the ruin palette.

## N6 — The Unquiet (the second extra: ghosts and curses)

The cheap-and-rich closer, riding grievance + sites + Works:

- **Ghosts:** a death with unresolved weight (murder unavenged, a leader
  betrayed, a family extinguished in a raid) can leave an unquiet dead at the
  site — a Being that does nothing but *be seen* (omens localize, scene texture
  darkens) until laid to rest (the grievance repaid, the Work returned, the rite
  performed — S4's reconciliation machinery gets a supernatural client).
- **Cursed Works:** the curse rider (W5) attaches to THINGS — the blade that
  ruins its bearers, provable in the custody chain ("all four holders died
  violently within a decade"). Harrenhal's "ruins with names and curses" at
  Work scale; dungeons gain treasure you should not take.

## Waves and order

| Wave | Tenant | Leans on |
|---|---|---|
| N1 | Vampires + self-bound undead (closes the Age-of-Crows seam) | Beings, Sites, bloodline mirror, eras |
| N2 | Arms & Scars (works-to-war, wounds, duels, veterancy) | Works, Mars, health, prestige |
| N3 | The Grimoire (spell-works, teaching chains, rituals) | Works, discovery table, E6 stamps |
| N4 | The Made (constructs, dormancy, the machine-craft category) | Works, Beings, E6 hands |
| N5 | The Salt Road (fishing, sea routes, isles, ships, wrecks) | Map/terrain, routes, fission, Beings |
| N6 | The Unquiet (ghosts, cursed Works) | Grievance, sites, curse riders, S4 |

N1–N4 are Ryan's named four, in dependency order (N3/N4 want E6's stamps landed).
N5–N6 are the volunteered extras — reorderable, droppable, or a second train.
Playbook full table per wave; every tenant's acceptance test is its chronicle:
the ancient who remembers the First Age, the blade with a legend, the lost spell
found, the sentinel over the dead town, the first sail, the blade you should not
take.
