# The Map — geography derived from the archetypes, worked backward into the model

**Date:** 2026-07-11
**Status:** Design (Luna-authored; Ryan's method: start from what makes the great
fantasy maps unique, then work those elements INTO the model — not "what can our
model already draw")
**Related:** the-wide-world.md, SpaceState/worldgen (Saturn's substrate), F5 routes,
W0–W3 beasts, W2 sites

## What the archetypes actually put on their maps

- **Lord of the Rings** — the map reads as a *journey*: mountain **ranges as
  walls** that divide the story (the Misty Mountains split the world; the
  plot IS getting across), **rivers as both roads and borders**, forests as
  places of otherness, singular landmarks (a lone mountain that means
  something), and regions whose names carry moral weather (the Shire vs
  Mordor). The cartography itself — hachured peaks, tree-cluster forests,
  labeled in a humanist hand — is half the identity.
- **Wheel of Time** — the **wall with one pass** (the Spine of the World: a
  barrier that makes a chokepoint, and a chokepoint makes a culture on each
  side), and the **danger gradient**: the Blight, a corrupted zone that
  begins where the map's colors sicken — distance-from-safety as a visible
  field. Also: vast *empty* stretches between cities — a depopulated world
  wearing the ruins of a greater age.
- **Malazan** — deep time on the map: **ruins of prior civilizations layered
  under the present** (barrows, dead cities, holy deserts that remember), and
  the honesty of the unmapped — the map understates the world.
- **A Song of Ice and Fire** — **geography as politics**: the Neck's swamp
  chokepoint deciding every war, river systems structuring campaigns, a
  north–south climate gradient hardening regional identities, islands making
  island cultures, and **ruins with names and curses** (Harrenhal — a place
  on the map whose history IS its danger).

## The elements, extracted — and seated in OUR model

| Element (from the masters) | What it does for a world | Model seat | Existing hooks it feeds |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Coastline & sea** | the map's silhouette IS the world's face; edges end somewhere | water terrain at worldgen (Saturn, seeded noise — coin 0's exemption) | settlement placement, future fishing; the viewer's identity shot |
| **Mountain ranges + passes** | walls that divide; chokepoints that make cultures and wars | impassable tract chains with seeded pass gaps | **routes must path through passes** (F5) → isolation → stance divergence (F2) → raid geography (F3) — the whole social engine gains terrain |
| **Rivers** | roads AND borders; civilizations grow on banks | linear features flowing highland→sea | tract fertility bonus on banks (existing multipliers), route bonus along/crossing |
| **Contiguous natural biomes** | forests/swamps/deserts with SHAPE and character, not grid confetti | seeded-noise biome fields replacing per-tract uniformity | biome multipliers exist; **beast biome affinity** (wolves in forests, bears in highlands) |
| **The danger pale** (Blight / beyond-the-Wall) | distance-from-civilization as a visible gradient | derived wilderness field (distance from communities/roads) | beast density, dungeon flavor, journey risk — mostly derivation + display |
| **Named geography** | "crossed the Weeping Pass" — labels are lore | GeoFeatures: named ranges/rivers/forests/seas/passes (name generator; light entities) | chronicle mentions, scene flavor, route descriptions |
| **Ruins with names on the map** | Harrenhal — history as a place | **already built** (W2 sites: ruins/towers/caves/shrines) | pure cartography: glyphs on the map |
| **Roads drawn** | the journey visible | **already built** (F5 routes) | draw them as paths |
| **The cartographic hand** | parchment, hachures, tree clusters, a compass rose, humanist labels | viewer-only | the minimap grows into THE MAP |

The pattern: the masters' elements split cleanly into **three that demand new
worldgen** (water, mountains+passes, rivers — all Saturn's creation act,
seeded, deterministic), **two that are derivation/config over what exists**
(contiguous biomes, the danger pale), and **three that are pure cartography**
(sites, routes, style) because this week already built their content.

## The two slices

**M1 — Geography (engine).** Worldgen terrain synthesis from the seed: noise-
shaped coastline (sea tracts), 1–2 mountain ranges as impassable chains with
seeded passes, rivers flowing from highlands to the sea, contiguous biome
fields; **GeoFeature naming** (ranges, rivers, forests, seas, passes); wired
to what exists — settlement placement avoids sea/peaks and favors banks,
tract fertility reads terrain, **route pathing respects walls and passes and
favors river valleys** (chokepoints emerge), beast spawning reads biome
affinity + the wilderness field. Config `world.geography.*` under coin 0.
Trajectories change everywhere (greenfield; golden re-records). The chokepoint
composition is the acceptance test: two regions divided by a range with one
pass should show route concentration, stance divergence across the wall, and
a pass community that matters.

**M2 — Cartography (viewer).** The minimap becomes the Map: terrain-rendered
(parchment ground, hachure-style peaks, tree-clustered forests, coast
outline, river threads), **site glyphs** (towns by size, ruins, towers,
shrines, dens, a dragon's lair), routes as drawn paths, the danger pale as a
wash, GeoFeature labels in a cartographic hand, zoom (world → region →
tract), clickable into the existing inspect flow. The map should pass the
glance test: *a stranger shown only the map should want to play in it.*
