Wednesday - 06/03/26
Log:
- Magnus3: Light research / orientation — placed the project's emerging design ("set initial values → iterate simulated time → apply modifiers → repeat, plus feedback that senses and adjusts values for specific entities") against established complex-systems modeling traditions, to gauge how general the approach is and which fields could lend insight.
  - Identified four traditions the design braids together: (1) **System Dynamics** (Forrester) — the core loop is its stock/flow/feedback-loop primitives almost verbatim; lineage Industrial Dynamics → Urban Dynamics (→ SimCity) → World3/Limits to Growth; gives vocabulary (balancing vs reinforcing loops) and named failure archetypes. (2) **Agent-Based Modeling** — the per-person, emergence-from-micro-rules half; canonical ref Epstein & Axtell *Growing Artificial Societies* (Sugarscape, strikingly close to Magnus3); tooling NetLogo / Mesa. (3) **Control theory / Cybernetics** — flagged as the most underused match: the Divine Domain Administration directives (deity senses a variable, compares to a goal like Population Homeostasis, adjusts knobs) *are* closed-loop controllers with setpoints (PID / governor / homeostat). (4) **Microsimulation** — population of individuals stepped through discrete periods via per-entity transition rules; the literal econ/epi/policy analog, with practical answers for scale, calibration, and aggregate *alignment*.
  - Versatility: confirmed broadly general — same skeleton underlies epidemiology (SIR/SEIR = stock-and-flow), ecology (Lotka-Volterra predator-prey, relevant to the Ecology layer), ABM economics, traffic, supply chains, climate. Noted the [[reference_exponator_tool]] seed × config sweeps are, in this lineage, **Monte Carlo ensemble simulation**.
  - Headline takeaway / next body of knowledge to import: the design's distinctive move is layering an **agent-level control system** (directive-driven deities) on a **system-dynamics substrate**. That hybrid's signature risk is *a controller acting on a noisy, delayed aggregate* → overshoot, oscillation, overshoot-and-collapse, integral windup — precisely the regime control theory exists to stabilize. Directly relevant to the [[project_divine_domain_administration]] directives (e.g. Fertility Restraint steering toward *resultant* CC, already flagged in Monday's review as manufacturing its own overhang). Recommended control theory (PID tuning, damping, delay/feedback coupling) as the next field to mine; offered a follow-up to map the actual layers (config knobs → modifiers → directives → ecology) onto stock/flow/controller vocabulary, or a focused brief on control-theoretic stability for the directive system.
