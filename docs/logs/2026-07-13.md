- **Culture review established the present boundary: E5 Peoples is a strong foreignness seed, not yet a culture system.** A live `ages_peoples`, seed-42 run crossed Elder Folk -> Men -> Elder Folk at years 40/80. The model produces real quantitative differences: separate personal/place phonotactics; Elder mean observed lifetime 62.7y vs Men's 20.2y in the 100-year cataclysm-heavy sample; and Elder specialist mix leaned toward Keeper/Adept as configured. Age residue, Works, ruins, maker provenance, and E6 make-stamps already preserve exactly the historical substrate a richer system needs.
  - The missing authorship is concrete. In a 300-year live export, Elder makers bore Elder names but their Works (`Brersbi`, `Daesbre`, `Laermmo`, etc.) were named from the shared world bank; `SitesSystem` explicitly passes `sc.world.names` to `work_name`. All peoples draw the same Work kinds, ship epithets, Site kinds, shrine form, and architectural vocabulary. Works/Sites have no material, form, motif, script, or cultural-signature fields. Stage currently infers "of the Elder Folk's make" by looking up the maker's `people` after the fact. The region `Culture` component is seeded once and has no reader anywhere in `src/`; it is currently dead data. Religion varies causally by god/domain/trial, but not culturally: one pantheon, one generic shrine relationship, no different theology or ritual grammar.
  - **Recommended ontology:** separate **species** (inherited body/life history), **people** (historical descent identity), **culture/tradition** (learned and transmissible practice), and **language** (its own genealogical tree). The current `People` record collapses all four: lifespan/fertility are species-like; calling affinities and material expression are cultural; the phoneme bank is language; the key itself is descent identity. Keeping them separate permits one species with many cultures, a multi-species culture, adoption/conquest, and a returning people whose later-age tradition/language is related but not identical.
  - **Recommended generative unit:** an age/community culture carries weighted ancestry plus 2-4 high-salience commitments, expanded through constrained domain grammars: motif subjects and meanings (real local animals/plants, celestial/ancestral/abstract signs); architecture (siting, plan, massing, openings, roof, ornament placement, construction technique); material hierarchy and local substitutions; religious ontology/access/rite/funerary expression; and per-Work-kind form/technique/ornament. Traits are distributions with prevalence and retention, not uniform labels. This yields internal variation while keeping a classifiable fingerprint.
  - **Language needs descent, not only alternate syllable bags.** Seed proto-roots for world concepts, derive daughter languages through deterministic sound-change sets, preserve old substrate place names, and allow bounded borrowing from predecessor/contact languages. Names and inscriptions then share morphemes, so parentage is both audible and queryable rather than decorative resemblance.
  - **The durable payoff is an immutable cultural make-signature.** At creation, each Work/Site should stamp culture, language, materials, form, motifs, technique, and any religious dedication beside the existing `(era, coin, level)` MakeStamp. Looting changes custody, not authorship. Ruins expose the signature of the town that raised them. Player lore can infer a likely tradition or mixed influence from observed clues instead of reading a maker lookup disguised as recognition.
  - **Suggested implementation order:** first uncollapse species/people/culture/language and generate one resolved culture/language lineage per configured age; then make Works and Sites consume and export cultural signatures (including culturally generated proper names); then render recognition in scene/lore/inspect surfaces; only after that add seasonal diffusion, adoption, conquest, or creolization. This first vertical slice is enough to produce the desired "this is unmistakably another people's work" discovery without putting cultural drift in every seasonal hot path.
  - **Quantifiable acceptance:** seeded determinism; local-material validity; no faceless/signatureless made things; within-culture variation; pairwise cultural distance floors on high-salience domains; a blind signature classifier that identifies the originating culture materially better than chance; and daughter-language/culture retention tests showing related ages closer to each other than to unrelated traditions without becoming copies.

That record is valuable and must remain queryable, but it should be cold archive, not next-age ECS state.
  - Present semantics are also too thin fictionally: absolute short boundary years; only `remnant` or `respawn`; remnant keeps the most populous towns; respawn creates founders from Heaven; every mortal in a selected town dies uniformly; all non-person beings carry indiscriminately; terrain cannot scar; gods and disposition simply persist; culture/species succession is configured rather than caused; and the apocalypse itself is not distinct from the successor policy. `WorldEnded`/Terminus is separately derived at export, so the world's ending does not author the next world's residue.

- **Recommended replacement architecture: one hot ECS per Age, with cold AgeArchives and a small ResidueManifest between them.** At roughly year 1,000, an ApocalypseResolver reads the settled age, resolves a short structured crisis (not thousands more seasonal ticks), writes the real AgeEnded record, freezes the complete age into an immutable compressed archive, extracts bounded residue, destroys the old Flecs world, and creates a fresh world from that manifest. The composed export/Stage can still query every archived person and event; systems never scan them. Cross-age continuity requires stable historical ids independent of Flecs ids. A persistent individual is re-incarnated as a fresh current-age entity carrying the same historical id and an archived tenure link.
  - Residue categories: a few **named survivors** (full person identity); **refugia** (aggregate species/people/culture/language ancestry that generates the next founders without carrying thousands of individuals); **persistent hosts** (an immortal race/army as one cohort plus named exemplars, the T'lan Imass shape); **bound threats** (sealed/waking powers); **world scars** (craters, flooded tracts, dead-magic zones, long winter, poisoned regions); **surface relics** (bounded current-age ruins/Works); and **latent strata** (the rest of the genuine archived ruins/Works, materialized when discovered rather than scanned seasonally). Gods get an explicit persistence/scarring policy rather than automatic carry.
  - Separate three questions currently collapsed into `succession`: **Apocalypse** (how the age dies), **Residue** (what crosses and in what state), and **Genesis** (refugia, transformed people, invader descendants, awakening elder folk, outside arrival, or explicit second creation). An invader can destroy an age and then itself collapse without becoming the successor; an immortal host can persist without being the new reproductive population.
  - Apocalypse archetypes should be data-backed resolver families composed from shared selectors/fronts/scars: (1) **The Tainted Gift**, where the strongest magic users become spatial calamity centers and surviving magic is cursed/lost; (2) **The Pale Host**, a geographic winter/undead front that leaves last holds, a sealed host, and cold scars; (3) **The Bright Ruin**, arcane-nuclear impact nodes that glass terrain and favor constructs/sealed beings; (4) **The Drowned Bronze**, sea invaders that break coastal routes and cities, briefly occupy, then die of famine/plague, leaving hybrid work, loanwords, and wreck strata; (5) **The Ritual of Dust**, a people deliberately transforms into a deathless host while its mortal civilization ends. The resolver should anchor origins in actual wizards, towers, routes, peoples, gods, or dooms when available, falling back to an explicit exogenous cause only when the world supplies none.
  - Proposed default timing: age target 1,000 years with modest deterministic variation (for example 900-1,100), optional configured duration, and a 10-30-year narrative crisis represented by 3-7 resolved phases (omens/breach/fall/silence), not a new permanent seasonal hot path. Accumulated Doom can choose or pull the apocalypse earlier; a scheduled end still occurs even in a calm age because apocalypse is the eon contract, not a failure state.
  - Acceptance should be mechanical: archive preserves 100% of prior record and checksum; transition removes at least 90-95% of hot entities; first-season table count/runtime is near a fresh world; zero ordinary prior-age events/persons remain in current queries; residue is bounded per region regardless of age count; default mortal named survivors are exceptional; immortal species/hosts obey explicit vulnerability rules; scars and survivor geography correlate with the apocalypse mechanism; successor ancestry is derived from refugia/invaders/transformations; and repeated runs produce the same archives, manifest, and next age.
  - Implementation sequence if approved: first build the age-shard/archive/manifest boundary with one intentionally plain Great Dying and prove the memory/runtime reset; then add stable historical ids, survivor/refugium/host categories and cultural ancestry; then scars + latent residue; finally the apocalypse registry and its first four resolver families, followed by Stage/viewer discovery surfaces. This should replace `EraDirector`, not accrete more branches into it.
