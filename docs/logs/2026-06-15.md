- **Magnus3: Established the Heaven/Earth scope as a binding architectural distinction.** Started from "wire the crowding on/off toggle," which surfaced a deeper question: founding fires on `alive >= crowd_fraction * natural_cc`, i.e. a community acting directly on its own carrying capacity — divine omniscience leaking into the world, unlike births (which a community never throttles itself; *Venus* administers fertility restraint). Traced every consumer of the computed CC value: it drives **nothing** mechanically — `ConsumptionSystem` (starvation/death) reads no CC, the base birth rate doesn't either; CC is consumed only by the god's administration (fertility restraint, `1.0` no-op when off), by the founding response, and by pure observation (gauge, graph, export, inspect). So CC's effects are wholly emergent from food accounting; the *number* is a legible projection, not load-bearing state. Generalized that into the system-wide axis (Ryan's framing, leaning Taoist 天地 / Hermetic "as above, so below"): **Earth** = the material world's substance and laws (food, bodies, fertility, lifespan, yield, family splitting); **Heaven** = what the gods see and the levers they hold (carrying capacity, crowding, the tech overlay, directive setpoints, the genesis schedule). The invariant, stated precisely (the relationship is two-way; the prohibition is not): *Heaven reads Earth freely* (its values are derived from earthly state) *and changes Earth only through a god's act; Earthly mechanics never read Heaven.* The one current breach is `process_founding` — the task that prompted all this; routing it through a Mercury directive is the fix (deferred, next).
