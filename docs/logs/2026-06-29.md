- **Theogonus Nova: another "from the beginning" pass — resolved the agency line, tyranny shape, and prestige; consolidated into the doc.** (Continues the design work from last week's `docs/research/2026-06-27-theogonus-nova.md`.)
  - **Agency line locked.** Saturn *sends* coins 1–2 (hunger, death); the community *chooses* coins 3+ (Leadership, Family, …). Two flavors of agency: *sent* coins → choose only the response (ascend or starve); *chosen* coins → choose whether to adopt at all. So **Leadership is offered, not inflicted.**
  - **Family ⟂ Leadership.** Reversed last week's hard-prereq: **neither requires the other** — both independent, chosen coins. Family's *curse* only **bites** once Community exists (privatization needs a pool to fragment; family alone, pre-community, is nearly inert) — an interaction, not a prerequisite. Keeping family optional is the point, so the no-family branch must stay coherent.
  - **Tyranny = neglect** (Ryan's reframe). Before families there's no kin to hoard *for*, so tyranny is the un-grounded leader **failing to do welfare distribution** — *who doesn't get fed* (dependents fall through the cracks). **Family escalates** it to favoritism (feed mine) and, via heritable prestige, to dynasty.
  - **Prestige = leader-selection currency.** Replaces today's *family-authority* value (disparity now lives in the leader's personality). Leader = highest-**prestige** individual; **Zeus tips the selection** toward non-drifters + steers the sitting leader (no direct appointment). Prestige is individual (dies with its holder) **until Family makes it heritable** → dynastic tyranny — family's curse stated mechanically.
  - **Family has no gather-style output** — it's a *structure*, not a labor role; what it "produces" is lineage + heritable prestige (clarified my earlier "mechanical output" question; a household-head role is possible but not adopted).
  - **Crowding/migration + agriculture = net positives** (no forced downside). **New families** spawned at pairing by chance × personality — maps onto the engine's existing **fission** logic.
  - **Live open question:** *what generates an individual's prestige* (contribution? personality? age?) — both selection and family's curse hang on it. Doc now reads **refactor-ready** (coins 0–4).
